- Machines backend: every Vyre daemon (the Space server, added servers, members' linked computers) reports its specs, load, what Vyre runs and what it can run every 30 seconds over the socket it already holds; the Space keeps one-minute points for 24 hours and ten-minute points for 30 days (a size budget test holds it). New `machines` module with `machines.list`, `machines.get`, `machines.set` and `machines.alerts`; an admin sees only name, online, capacity and Vyre's own work on a member's own computer.
- Placement skips a machine with no room (busy turn limit, CPU, memory, low disk, draining) for the next one in the same order and says why; room left is one number shared by the Machines page and placement.
