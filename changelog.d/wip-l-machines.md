- Machines: one backend, the `machines` module (`machines.list`, `get`, `set`, `alerts`, `rooms`, `fix`, `moves`, `move_record`, `alerts_notify`). Every Vyre daemon (the Space server, added servers, members' linked computers) reports its specs, load, what Vyre runs and what it can run every 30 seconds; the Space measures latency with a ping. One-minute points for 24 hours, then ten-minute points for 30 days, with a size budget test. An admin sees only name, online, capacity and Vyre's own work on a member's own computer.
- Machines: room left (`lib/room-left.ts`) is the one number the page and placement use. Automatic skips a machine with no room for the next one and says why ("Moved from X because it was busy: 4 of 4 turns running"); an agent pinned to a machine runs there first and moves on, with the reason, when it has no room or is off; a chat's own pin still wins. Moves are listed Space-wide under Recent moves.
- Machines: `machines.get` lists what runs on the machine (live turns from the engine, the Space server's sites and automations; never a message or title), the machine's own findings with a Fix (clear caches, stop idle engine sessions, restart a service) run by the host, and the last fix. The Space server's runs.sites and runs.automations are real counts.
- Machines: `machine.changed` is emitted on a real change of Drain or limits and the moment a machine runs out of room or has room again (when a turn starts or ends on it), so the per-chat placement cache is dropped and never ignores room.
- Console and Settings read machines.* (apps/app/src/machines/api.ts); the network module's machines capabilities and the second sampler are gone.
