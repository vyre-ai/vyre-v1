- When several people use one shared agent, each person who does not own it now works in a folder of their own in its computer, with their own shell history, settings and terminal sessions, so one person's files, history and open sessions are no longer there for another person's turn.
- When several people use one shared agent, each person who does not own it now browses in a browser profile of their own, so the sites another person signed in to are not signed in for them. The agent's owner keeps the agent's own browser profile with everything signed in before. What a person's turn downloads goes into that person's own Files.
