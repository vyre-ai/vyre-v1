A new tab finds places, chats, channels, records, files and people with the same search as Cmd-K: the best match is selected and Enter opens it in that tab (a chat starts only when nothing matches), and the new tab keeps the sidebar column. With many tabs none shrinks to an icon, a tabs menu lists every tab, closing a tab returns to the one you were on, and Shift-Cmd-T brings it back. The Inbox header names the list you chose, the phone's You tab has no blank hole, and the browser's Back on the phone stays in the app.
