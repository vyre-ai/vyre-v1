- In the desktop app, when Vyre is not in front, a new notice shows a system banner that says only "<Space> needs you": no name, chat or words from the notice. There is no banner while the window is in front, at most three a minute and then one summary, and one per notice (a notice that grows with more replies is not bannered again). Pressing a banner brings Vyre forward; it does not jump to a place on its own.
- The dock or taskbar badge shows your Needs you count, clears with it, and clears when you sign out or switch Space. If the system turned Vyre's banners off, Settings, Notifications says so once.
- Email notices go out from your Space's own sending mailbox on a verified domain: the Space deletion notices to each admin, and the approval reminder. The subject starts with the Space's name, the text is Vyre's own wording with the date, and the one link opens the Space in the Vyre app.
- With no sending mailbox nothing is emailed, and each admin's Console says so once: "Notice email is off: this Space has no sending mailbox". Console, Messaging, Email, Sending domains says it too.
- A notice email that failed is tried again every ten minutes, a few times; if it still fails, each admin's Console names the person it could not reach. A deletion that was cancelled is never followed by its "scheduled" email.
- Only Vyre itself sends the Space deletion notices and its other system notices (approval reminders, health, incidents). A person or an agent can send only notices about themselves talking to someone, and the notice names who really sent it.
- The Space owner always hears about the Space being scheduled for deletion, even when a role cannot be read. A link to Console opens it only for people Console lets in; anyone else lands in their Inbox.
- A schedule, a template or a Kit that runs a step for you runs it with your own standing, so it cannot send a notice only Vyre may send. An agent's notice tool now offers only the notices an agent may send.
