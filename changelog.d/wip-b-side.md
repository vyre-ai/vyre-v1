- The Chats sidebar has a new model behind it: needs you, now, pinned, chats, channels and team, each capped with an "N more" that shows what it hides, and one mark per row.
- Every row in the new Chats sidebar has a right-click menu with the same items its keys reach; the word is Pin. A row says what it is by its shape (person, agent, Assistant, group, channel, project, goal) and wears one mark.
- New keys, listed in Settings under Keyboard: Cmd-N new chat, Shift-Cmd-N new group chat, Option-Cmd-N new channel, Option-Cmd-G new goal, Option-Cmd-T new task, Option-Cmd-O new note, Option-Cmd-1 to 5 for the places, Cmd-Shift-backslash to hide the sidebar, Option-Shift-Down and Up for the next and previous unread chat.
- The rail has one word under each place (Chats, Inbox, Records, Files, Automations), the foot button says More, and its dot is the accent colour. The rail is a little wider to fit "Automations".
- The Chats sidebar has a Goals section again under Tasks (active goals first, with the lead's face in a progress ring, then quiet ones), and Tasks folds like every other section: the arrow folds it, the name opens Tasks, and the count stays when folded.
- In the new sidebar Team lists the whole team, most recent first; a one-to-one chat is that person's row in Team, not a second row in Chats.
- A chat you started typing in keeps your unsent line on this computer and comes back when you open it; the sidebar marks such a chat with a pencil. It is cleared when you send, and is not shared across your devices.
- Keys that changed meaning in the new sidebar: Cmd-1 to Cmd-9 now open your first nine pinned rows (before: your first nine starred agents); Cmd-Backslash to hide the sidebar becomes Cmd-Shift-Backslash. Cmd-T (new tab), Cmd-D, Option-Down (Inbox next unread) are unchanged.
- A chat's mention count can now be read on its own: a new read lists the mentions you have not seen, per chat or channel, only yours.
- A list that fails to load in one section shows one quiet line with Retry instead of a big error card; "N more" and "Show less" under a capped list are one shared row with Enter, Right and Left keys.
- The Chats sidebar can be read in one answer: your needs, goals, pinned, chats, channels and team with their marks, a page at a time.
- The Chats sidebar opens fast however many chats you have: one page of 50 is read without reading every chat, and a person with 5,000 chats and 200 channels gets the first page in under 50 ms.
- The Chats sidebar is the new one: Tasks (which folds), Goals, Pinned, Chats, Channels and Team, one mark on each row, a menu on every row, and one word under each place in the rail. It loads in one read.
- Option-Command-1 to 5 go to the first five places of the rail.
- Sidebar rows show only their name and one mark at rest; the three dots appear on hover or keyboard focus. A row is bold only when something is new in it, and the pinned rows show their Cmd digit only while you hold Cmd.
- You can rename a chat and add people to it from its row in the sidebar; a goal's row says why it waits or when it next checks in, and Report progress and Mark stuck work from it.
- Open "N more" in the sidebar to reach every chat, not only the first 50. Finish setup now sits at the foot of the sidebar, Notes & artifacts is one page with a Notes and Artifacts switch that remembers the tab, and the Chats number on the rail is the same number the sidebar adds up.
- All text in the app uses one font family; on computers without Inter the rail words and the list titles were two different fonts.
- An archived chat and an imported coding session can always be found: the filter menu lists Archived and Imported (Imported in a Personal Space), and each list loads past its first 50 rows. People and agents beyond the first 200 are read as well.
- Unsent text is kept for each person and each Space on a computer, so two people sharing one browser never see each other's lines or pencils. Signing out of a Space clears the lines kept for it.
- The Chats sidebar read fails closed: if the Space cannot say who is asking, the caller is treated as a guest. A paging key that is not one of your own chats now gives the first page instead of reading your whole list, and the chat id of an agent's work in a chat you are not in no longer leaves the presence read.
- A busy Space no longer makes the Chats sidebar read again on every change: the sidebar and the dots for who is around and which agents work are each read at most once a second (before, up to 8 a second for each, about 480 a minute). Your own actions, like pinning or renaming, are still read at once. An idle Space reads nothing.
- Walk proving an archived chat (Team and Personal Spaces) and a chat past the first 50 can be found and opened from the sidebar.
- The unsent-text store of earlier versions (one shared key for everyone on a computer) is deleted the first time this version opens; its text is not moved into anyone's chats. Unsent text is kept per person, and signing out of a Space clears that person's lines wherever the app runs, in a browser or the desktop app.
- Read rows in the Chats sidebar have their title in the second ink, and only unread rows (bold) and the selected row keep the full ink. The compact Couldn't load line with Retry now draws in a section of a list.
- In the Chats sidebar a goal that waits on you shows once, in Tasks, and its Goals row has no second line. Tasks rows have the same height as chat rows, and a row cut by the Notes and artifacts foot fades out.
- A goal waiting on you is matched to its Tasks row by the goal itself, not by title, so a second goal with the same name keeps its line. The Unblock task a stuck goal makes now says which goal it is about.
- A stuck goal made before this version still shows once: its Unblock task names no goal, so it is matched to the goal by its title.
