- What each person has read, finished, starred, followed or muted now has a place of its own on the server, so one person's reading can never clear another's. Nothing changes on screen yet; the Inbox moves onto it next.
- In a shared Inbox, what you have read is yours alone: reading a conversation no longer marks it read for your teammates, and the unread numbers on the Inbox lists and counts are your own. A conversation you mark done leaves your lists only, and comes back when a new message arrives. Upgrading keeps what was read: mail anyone had read counts as read for everyone who can see it.
