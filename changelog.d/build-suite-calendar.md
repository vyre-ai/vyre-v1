- Google and Outlook calendars now come into Calendar. Connecting a member (delegation or consent setup, or including them again) puts their primary Google or default Outlook calendar in Calendar, owned by them and private until they share it, and each mailbox pass asks Calendar to sync it, read as their own account. Excluding a member removes their calendar and its events from Calendar. New capability calendar.connect_provider (admin only).
- Calendars are now access resources from the moment they are made. Before, a calendar made in Calendar (or connected from Google or Outlook) answered "not found" to Sync now, Rename and Add event, even for its owner, because nothing registered it. New event calendar.created; an admin who connects a member's Google or Outlook calendar does not become its owner, the member does.
