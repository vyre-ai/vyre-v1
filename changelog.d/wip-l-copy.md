## What's new

- A goal's Activity now reads in plain words and credits the right name. "Dex drafted this goal for you" is credited to Dex, not to "Irfad Imtiaz (Agent)". Times show once, in your own time zone, and a deadline in the finish line no longer repeats.
- One word catalog for activity lines and error messages (lib/words). Errors that came with no message, or one with a code or an id in it, now say what happened and what to do next.
