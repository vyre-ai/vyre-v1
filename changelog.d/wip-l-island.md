## What's new
- A pill floats above the chat composer while anyone is working: the Vyre mark, the live state word (Thinking, Writing, Reading, Searching the web), and one face per working agent with its state ring. Press a face to steer that agent, press the pill for the list of everyone working with background jobs and Stop, and press the computer button to watch the computer while an agent uses it.
- The Island turns into the question when an agent asks you something: the asker's face with an amber ring, the question, its choices as buttons (the recommended one filled), Other for a few words of your own, and who can answer. Press a choice or its number to answer and the pill goes back to Working. "1 of 3" and arrows walk several questions. Escape puts the pill away and the question stays in the chat. On a phone the pill is full width and the choices wrap.
- An agent's question can name the option it recommends (ask_question takes recommended, the position of that option); the Island draws that choice as the main one. A question without it falls back to the option whose note starts with Recommended.
