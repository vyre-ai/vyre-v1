The new chat composer can now show every address you can send from in one list: your Gmail and Outlook mailboxes, shared, agent and service inboxes, the Space sending domain (Resend or Mailgun) and your texting numbers (Twilio). Senders that cannot send right now are listed with the reason in plain words (paused, disconnected, receive only, no verified domain), and ones you may not use are not shown. Pick a default sender for email and for text; it is kept in your own settings. A send from the list is checked again when it goes out, and refused with a plain reason if the sender was disconnected or you lost access in the meantime. The model panel gets real usage for each account: how full the chat is against the model's window, and your session and weekly limits for Claude subscriptions, shown only when Claude reports them (nothing is estimated, and a provider that reports nothing shows no meter). Two accounts of one provider now appear under the names you gave them in the Vault.
