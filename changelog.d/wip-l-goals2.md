## What's new
- A goal's helpers now reach its chat: the goal chat may gain people (it was refused as a private chat), so the owner and helpers are added, and a plan step added to a draft goal waits in the drafted plan until Start instead of failing. Making or changing a goal now reports what happened to each helper, and a failure is written to the goal's activity.
- The goal page edits in place. Click the title or the finish line to retype it, the deadline for a date and time field, the lead, the owner or the helpers for the people picker, a checklist item or a drafted plan step to retype, drag, remove or add. Every edit is one change to the goal with Undo, and it shows in the goal's activity. Helpers show on the page with their names and a remove. The finish line no longer repeats the deadline, and a draft shows its drafted plan.
