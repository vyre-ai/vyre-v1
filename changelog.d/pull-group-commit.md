A sync pull makes new records a group of up to 200 at a time in one commit instead of one commit each: a first sync of thousands of records is faster, and the records, links, events and report it leaves are the same. A group that fails as a whole is made one row at a time, so one bad row never takes its group with it. A process kill between a group's commit and its links makes the next pass make up to 200 again (before: one).
