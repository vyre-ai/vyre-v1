- A guest of a channel can now open it, read what was said, and (at reply level) post and open a thread. An agent does not answer a guest. Before, the channel was listed but every open was refused with "You can't open this chat". A guest still sees and opens only the channels they were added to.
- A guest sees the names of the people and agents in their channels, not "Someone", and nobody else is listed.
- A guest's sidebar no longer offers People and Agents (a chat with either is refused them), and their start page says to pick a channel instead of offering a chat that cannot start.
- Channels made before this release open for guests too: the first time one is used, its chat is moved under its channel.
- A guest of a channel reads and replies in the threads under its messages too, at the level they were invited at; a thread an agent or a person started from the channel (a helper, a handoff, an Ask pane, an aside) stays closed to them.
- A guest of a channel reads, reacts, replies and opens reply threads, and nothing else: stopping the agents, starting helpers or handing work on, changing modes, pinning, renaming and adding people are refused them, including for guests invited before this release.
- A guest who mentions an agent in a channel is not answered by it: the mention reaches the channel, and one quiet line says agents answer members of the Space. The People list of a chat names its people and agents for a guest without offering to open them or add anyone.
- A guest whose access to a channel is removed no longer sees it in their lists, and reads or writes nothing in it, including the reply threads they opened. A role made from Guest is held to the same limits as Guest, and a guest made a member is treated as a member from their next message. Members of a channel marked with a label keep reading its chat.
