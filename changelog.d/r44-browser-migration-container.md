- A Space's saved browser sign-ins are now copied by a one-shot migration container (old shared volume read-only, the Space's own volume read-write, no network) and the Space's long-running browser container mounts only its own volume, never the old shared one that holds every Space's cookies. The browser waits for an unfinished copy instead of starting without a sign-in.
- The reconnect wait for the browser and terminal views now grows to 2 s at most (was 8 s); the 30 s give-up and Try again are unchanged. After the daemon is killed under an open terminal, the stream is back in about 6 s (was about 8 s).
