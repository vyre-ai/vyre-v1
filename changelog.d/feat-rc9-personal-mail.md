A person can sign in to their own Gmail or Outlook, in any Space, not only through a Workspace. Add an OAuth app to the Vault (a Google client or a Microsoft app registration with client_id and client_secret, named for example Google_Sign_In), name it in Settings > Connections > Sign in with your own account, which shows the exact redirect address to register, and then Connect a mailbox in the Inbox, Settings or any picker shows Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft. The person's refresh token is sealed in the Google or Microsoft module and is in no Vault item, no answer and no event; a mailbox poll with nobody signed in gets its access token from it, and no one else can use it. The mailbox is a normal mailbox: the first pull takes the last 90 days, later pulls follow Gmail history ids or Graph delta links, mail.send_as sends through the person's own Gmail or Graph, and disconnecting removes the account and tells Google to revoke the token. Microsoft personal accounts are read through Graph /me. Without an app in the Vault the Connect a mailbox sheet is unchanged. New capabilities: google.user.app_status, app_set, start, complete, list, remove; ms.user.app_status, app_set, start, complete, list, remove; new events google.user_connected and microsoft.user_connected; mail.connect takes an optional connectionId (generated files need regenerating).
