A person can sign in to their own Gmail or Outlook, in any Space, not only through a Workspace. Add an OAuth app to the Vault (a Google client or a Microsoft app registration with client_id and client_secret, named for example Google_Sign_In), name it in Settings > Connections > Sign in with your own account, which shows the exact redirect address to register, and then Connect a mailbox in the Inbox, Settings or any picker shows Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft. The person's refresh token is sealed in the Google or Microsoft module and is in no Vault item, no answer and no event; a mailbox poll with nobody signed in gets its access token from it, and no one else can use it. The mailbox is a normal mailbox: the first pull takes the last 90 days, later pulls follow Gmail history ids or Graph delta links, mail.send_as sends through the person's own Gmail or Graph, and disconnecting removes the account and tells Google to revoke the token. Microsoft personal accounts are read through Graph /me. Without an app in the Vault the Connect a mailbox sheet is unchanged. In a Team Space the mailbox is private to its owner (in a Personal Space it files like any other). Settings > Connections > Sign in with your own account is a guided panel: the steps at Google or Microsoft, the exact redirect address with Copy, and the client id and secret, which Vyre files in a Shared Apps folder in the Vault. When the provider stops accepting the saved sign-in (revoked, expired), the mailbox shows Needs attention, the owner gets one Needs you item with Sign in again (also on the mailbox row in Settings), polling for it stops, and signing in again to the same address closes the item and starts the pull again. New capabilities: mail.mailbox.set_sync_state, google.user.app_status, app_set, start, complete, list, remove; ms.user.app_status, app_set, start, complete, list, remove; new events google.user_connected and microsoft.user_connected; mail.connect takes an optional connectionId (generated files need regenerating).

A person's assistant now acts with its owner's access to the owner's mailbox, up to Use (an assistant reads "my inbox"), and with nobody else's; the admin's assistant reads no member's mailbox. A mailbox made by a person's own sign-in sends through new google.user.send_message and ms.user.send_message (the provider caps are bound to a connection and refuse these ids). The removal wizard's revoke of mailbox connections also ends the person's own sign-ins and their tokens. Two Needs you items that were never created are fixed: the access change proposal (a review task offered done and decline) and the spend limit card (an unblock task offered raise_once and stop) now use the action keys their kind takes, and a test holds every tasks.create in the modules to its kind's keys. New capabilities: google.user.send_message, ms.user.send_message (generated files need regenerating).

A person can now open their own mail in the Inbox. Opening any conversation crashed the Inbox place (the conversation view called hooks after an early return, React error 310), a conversation from a sender with no record could not be opened or marked read (the access rules hold no row for it, and the five conversation capabilities were bound to one), and a Team member's private mailbox showed an empty Inbox because private mail was never linked. Now: the view calls its hooks before any return, with a test that scans every component for the pattern; inbox.thread, mark_read, match, unmatch, match_new and match_suggestions follow the same visibility rule as the list (owner, follower, campaign owner, the inbox it arrived in) and are bound to the Space; mail from a private mailbox is linked into a conversation of its owner's own, matched to no record and merged with nothing, so the owner sees it in the Inbox and nobody else does (inbox.link_message takes private and ownerId). A Personal Space's Home shows Needs you when something waits, and a lost mailbox sign-in's button opens Connect a mailbox right there, which finishes when the mailbox is well again. The redirect address in the sign-in app guide is shown in full. Manifest changes: inbox.link_message input, inbox.mark_read level view, the conversation capabilities bound to the Space (generated files need regenerating).
