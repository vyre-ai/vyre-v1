Provider all-day events (dates kept as midnight UTC) are drawn on their own date in the viewer's zone instead of across two days, and the pane reads their date the same way.
- Calendar: a meeting that sits in several people's calendars (each attendee's copy has the provider's same event id) is listed once in a week: the copy on the viewer's own calendar, else the first one they can open.
