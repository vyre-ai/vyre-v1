- You can now type into an agent's terminal yourself: open the terminal from the agent's Computer tab, press Take over, and what you type goes to the terminal. Everyone watching sees who is typing and cannot type, the agent's own commands are refused until you hand the keyboard back (or 15 quiet minutes pass), and everything you type is on the audit log as yours, never as the agent's. The Space server's setting VYRE_TERMINAL_TAKEOVER=0 turns it off.
- Keys you type are sent once: when a connection drops and the app tries again, the terminal never runs a command twice, a late retry never lands in what the agent runs after you gave the keyboard back, and a batch that went missing is refused in plain words instead of being skipped.
