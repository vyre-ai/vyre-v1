An existing text, email, phone, number or link field can be sealed. Sealing asks for a yes and says it is one way: every value moves into the Vault (the Sealed fields folder), the record keeps a reference, and the old plaintext is taken out of the record history, the Space log (the events keep their place and lose their payloads), the sync copies, kept answers of old writes and the search index. If the Vault stops answering partway, nothing in the Space changes and pressing Seal again carries on; if the cleaning is interrupted, the next boot finishes it. Sealed values can be revealed by the owner and by anyone with the Reveal vault values permission; each reveal is logged.
