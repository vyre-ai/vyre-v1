A fill made with no key of its own, including documents.make, is keyed by the template and its version, the values, the date, the folder and the person, so a retry is the same document; after the template is edited the same ask is a new document, by design.
A copy that is not the exact one can be sent anyway only by a person at the top of the call: an agent, someone's agent, or an Automation step is refused and asked to bring a person in.
A fill with no key of its own is also keyed by the record, and by the record's values as read, so another record or a corrected record is a new document. A held send of a person's own that the Gate resumes after they press Allow keeps their choice to send a copy that is not the exact one.
documents.make with no key is keyed by what the document will say as the record stands, so another record or a corrected record is a new document. Choosing to send a copy that is not the exact one is decided by who called: a person's own call, or their own held send resumed by the Gate (gate.release), never an agent or an Automation step (named by the calling capability, not by nesting depth).
The approval card of a held signature send of a preview copy says so (title and a server-built sentence). An Automation step never carries the choice to send a copy that is not the exact one, so a preview copy in a step is held back whoever approves the step.
documents.make with no key is keyed on every resolved value of the template, headers and footers included, and the template version it was read at (one read), not on the capped excerpt; preview_fill now answers a digest and the template version.
The approval card of a held send of a preview copy now shows the server's sentence quietly under the title and the names it goes to apart from it (names cleaned, never as sentences). A sign step on a file that is not the exact Word copy stops in the step's own words.
The keyless key of documents.make folds in a hash of the template's own bytes (read once with the preview), not a version number that may be missing; preview_fill's digest covers it.
The preview-copy note also shows on the Needs-you detail (one shared note component with the chat card), and a held signature send is drawn as its own lines there.
Only the Gate or the server can make a held act: a task naming a gate source, or writing the reserved decision and payload-hash notes, is refused for anyone else (checked by the pipeline's own caller capability). The cards show the Gate's note only on a task made by the Gate (source and payload hash).
