Agents now start with a real AGENT.md. Every new agent gets four short parts: Use me when (from its role line, never just the role word), How I work, What I never do and How I speak. Your private assistant gets one written for a chief of staff, under its own name ("Irfad’s Assistant"): it plans your day, finds and summarises, drafts and sends for your yes, keeps records and files tidy, sets up automations, chases follow-ups and remembers what matters. It acts without asking on anything inside Vyre that can be undone, and asks once before anything leaves Vyre. Every file passes the AGENT.md checks well under the 150-line limit. Agents and assistants whose AGENT.md is still the untouched old starter get the new text as a new version at start; a file anyone edited is never touched.
