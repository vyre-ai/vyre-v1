- The desktop app joins and signs in to a Space server on a vyre.run name, such as mla.vyre.run. It used to refuse any `<name>.vyre.run` address with "This Space is reached through Vyre's relay". The app now asks the address: a JSON `/healthz` that names a Space id with the space role, or the Space's sign-in options, comes only from a Space server, since the relay forwards neither. A relay phone address, an address that does not answer and a probe that times out keep the relay message. A direct answer is kept for the session, and an own domain such as vyre.example.com is never asked.
- On a direct vyre.run Space, the browser's invite and owner-claim pages offer "Open in Vyre" once the page's own origin answers as a Space. A page under the relay phone shell's service worker never does.
- `/healthz` carries the desktop app's CORS header (packaged origins only), so the app can read it. Servers from before this release prove themselves through the sign-in options instead.
