Work now passes a baton in a task thread, and agents wake when it reaches them. Every task has its own thread, the one place for its brief, hand-overs and fixes; it stays out of Recent chats, and the people and agents on the task (Assigned, Reviewer, Approver) are in it. Agents get five tools to end their part: task_submit hands in the outputs with a one-line summary (checked against the schema the task asks for), task_ask puts a question to the requester or a named person and the work waits for the answer, task_block reports what is in the way, task_review approves or sends work back with numbered fixes (after the review loop limit a person decides), and task_decide approves or rejects with a reason. When the baton lands on an agent, it is woken in the task thread and told its role, what the task is and which tool to finish with; a group or role is offered to its agent members and the first to take the task holds it; a finished question wakes the work that waited on it. Each hand-over is also written as a line in the thread. An agent that ends its turn without handing the task on is nudged once in the thread and woken again; if the task still has not moved after the step's nudge time (30 minutes by default), a person gets a task in Needs you, the one the step names or the person who asked. A Run waiting on a task now goes on when the task is Done or Canceled by any route, not only when it is approved or answered. The Create a task step can wait for its task and go on with what the doer handed in (its outputs and summary).
