- The desktop app updates itself. When it opens, the shell checks its channel with an 8 second timeout; the page waits up to 2.5 seconds for the answer before the lock screen. A newer signed version, with "Update Vyre automatically" on (the default), shows a full-window Updating Vyre screen: the Vyre mark, "Updating to <version>", a thin bar with the real bytes and percent, and calm words. The shell downloads it, checks the minisign signature against the pinned release key, waits until no chat turn runs on this computer, stops every vyred daemon and waits for its process to end, installs (the Mac bundle swap, or the NSIS installer on Windows) and restarts. Offline, a failed or slow check never holds the start. A failed install says so in one line with "Open Vyre anyway", starts the daemons again, and the next start tries again.
- Before it stops a daemon the update asks it what runs: GET /local/daemon/busy and POST /local/daemon/drain (loopback and the admin token only, host/src/routes/local-work.ts). The engine module reports its running turns through its seam; a source that can hand its turns to the Space's server (a person's Team Space link, lane l-runson) adds itself with a handOver and moves them on drain, and the rest are waited for, up to ten minutes, after which the update waits for the next start.
- Settings has About Vyre: the version, the channel, when it last checked, Check now, "Update Vyre automatically" and What's new. With the toggle off a newer version waits there as a quiet "Update available" row with Update now, and Settings has a dot on the rail until it installs. The update section moved here from This computer.
- What's new: on the first open after an update, one sheet, "What's new in Vyre", with the release's own lines and Continue. It shows once per version, never on a fresh install, and opens again from About Vyre. The web app and the phone show it too: they are the new version as soon as they load.
- Release pipeline: a changelog.d fragment may carry a "## What's new" part, plain lines for people. scripts/changelog/assemble.ts collects them into apps/app/public/whats-new.json (refusing more than six lines, an em dash or a long line), cli.ts bump stamps its version, and the sign job publishes them as the desktop feed's notes. Each desktop signature's trusted comment now names its release (version:<v>), so the app refuses a feed that pairs a new version with an older signed bundle.
- desktop.yml proves it on a real Mac and a real Windows runner (apps/desktop/src-tauri/ci/update-proof.ts), on the installed app started the way a person starts it: a feed of the proof's own offers the published 1.0.0-rc.18 bundles as a newer version; a bad signature is refused with nothing installed, then the signed update installs with the daemons stopped first and the new app opens. Built apps under test set VYRE_NO_AUTO_UPDATE=1, so no CI run updates itself.

## What's new

- Vyre now keeps itself up to date. When a new version is ready, it updates as it opens and you are back in a moment.
- A calm Updating screen shows how far along it is, and your chats and files stay just as they are.
