- Agents now start every turn knowing where they are. The turn carries an ordered context: who the agent is (its role and AGENT.md), the Space, this chat and who is in it by name, the memory block, the Working in Vyre manual and an index of other skills, a map of what it can do here (tool limits, the Vault items it may use by handle, its routines and watchers, its computer), what is on the person's screen, the time in their zone and what it waits on. API-key chats get it as system blocks, Claude Code and Codex get it in front of the message, and a resumed engine session is sent only what changed. Chat history shows names instead of user ids.
- A message can carry where the person sent it from (`chat.post` takes `screen`: the place, what is open and selected, filters, a summary of the rows). The agent gets it with the focus skill of that place. `chat.screen.get` reads it back for the person who sent it.
- "Working in Vyre" is now a manual of the whole product, with a focus skill per place (Records, Files, Automations, Vault, Inbox, Apps, Console) and two for the agent itself (its own loops, its computer). An agent loads any built-in skill by name with `agents_builtin_skill_get`. Every tool a skill names is checked against the real tool list, and 12 new scenarios replay the skills against it.
- An agent can set, list, change and stop its own schedules and watchers from chat (`agents_routine_save`, `agents_routines_list`, `agents_routine_delete`). They show on its page and in the chat's Routines tab. Cron runs in UTC and the skill and the context say so.
- A 30-task chat benchmark on a seeded Space scores a turn with and without the context by checks on the resulting state: 20 of 30 without, 29 of 30 with. It runs with `node --experimental-strip-types test/chat-brain.benchmark.ts`, and each side runs as a test.
