- Members of a Team Space on a Vyre Server can use their own Claude, ChatGPT or Grok account, in the browser and the desktop app. Signing in with your password opens your Vault on the Space for as long as you stay signed in, with no separate Unlock step; signing out closes it. Before, Vault said "Your Mine vault opens on the computer your Space runs on, not from here" and nothing could be connected.
- When your own account cannot open (you signed out, or the server restarted), your chat runs on the Space AI key and says so in one line, instead of waiting on a locked Vault.
- If you sign in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub, your own AI accounts open with nothing to type. After you have typed your password once (when you join, or at one password sign-in), the server keeps your Vault key sealed under its own secret and opens it when your provider proves it is you. Every such open is in the audit log. Vault > AI providers shows it under your own sign-ins, with "Forget on this server".
- Console > Policies > Sign-in requirements has "Members' AI sign-ins: Open after Google or GitHub sign-in", on by default, and says what keeping the key costs. Turning it off deletes every kept key, and members then type their password once per device.
