An export of records can be as large as the upload limit (25 MB) instead of 256 KB: Meier's 28,000 contacts export as csv or Excel; one over 25 MB is refused saying what the limit is and what to do.

The file of a big export is built a few hundred rows at a time with a turn for other requests between, and a file over the upload limit says so in the same words for everyone; only an export of records is also told to use fewer rows or columns.
