- Agents and automations now have an AI account choice, built on the Space AI accounts the Vault already keeps. The New agent page and the agent page's AI accounts list get an AI account row: Automatic (the cheapest Space AI account kept for that agent's own work) or one Space AI account by name. It pays for what the agent does with nobody asking, routines, watchers and helpers; in a chat the person's own account still comes first. An automation's Settings get an AI account group with a main and a backup, used by every AI step that does not choose its own. An account only pays when the Vault says it is kept for that work (Automations, or this agent's own work) and it is under its monthly cap; otherwise it is passed over, the next account that is kept for it takes the work, and the refusal names the account and what to tick when nothing can pay.
- A Space automation's AI step no longer needs a key picked in every step: with none chosen it runs on the automation's AI account, or on Automatic, the cheapest Space AI account kept for automations. Turning the automation on still says what to add when no Space AI account is kept for automations. The step's "Runs on" picker now offers Automatic in place of "No key".
- The AI account picker offers only Space AI accounts that work and are kept for that work (automations, or this agent's own work), each with its monthly cap. When none is kept for it, the picker says so in one line and links to Vault, AI accounts.
- An agent's Chat key (one Space account paying for every person's chats) is now a separate, clearly labelled choice under AI account on the agent page: "Pay for chats with this account instead of each person's own". It is off for every agent unless someone sets it, and only people who manage the Space's AI accounts can set it; others are refused with the reason. With it off, the asker's own account answers a chat first.
- A turn's Details now say who paid and why: "Paid by" names the account and says when it is the agent's Chat key, a Space AI account, or the asker's own account.
- The prototype copy used for lane screenshots (scripts/shots/prototype/vyre-v1.html) is refreshed from the current prototype, so it has the AI account scenes.
- Each turn now keeps who paid and why, written when the turn ends from the rule that chose the account: the agent's Chat key, the account picked for the chat, the agent's own-work account, a step's account, a Space AI account, a backup, or the asker's own key or sign-in. It shows as "Paid because" in the reply's Details tab and hover card (for the asker and AI-spend managers), and engine.turn.get reads it from the stored turn. Older turns show nothing. It is kept with the turn's other Details, so no database change is needed.
- The Chat key on the agent page is now an On/Off switch with a line saying what it does; the account menu appears only when it is on. The picker's "nothing kept" row is short ("None kept for this agent") so it fits the menu.
