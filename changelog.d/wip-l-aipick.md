- Agents and automations now have an AI account choice, built on the Space AI accounts the Vault already keeps. The New agent page and the agent page's AI accounts list get an AI account row: Automatic (the cheapest Space AI account kept for that agent's own work) or one Space AI account by name. It pays for what the agent does with nobody asking, routines, watchers and helpers; in a chat the person's own account still comes first. An automation's Settings get an AI account group with a main and a backup, used by every AI step that does not choose its own. An account only pays when the Vault says it is kept for that work (Automations, or this agent's own work) and it is under its monthly cap; otherwise it is passed over, the next account that is kept for it takes the work, and the refusal names the account and what to tick when nothing can pay.
- A Space automation's AI step no longer needs a key picked in every step: with none chosen it runs on the automation's AI account, or on Automatic, the cheapest Space AI account kept for automations. Turning the automation on still says what to add when no Space AI account is kept for automations. The step's "Runs on" picker now offers Automatic in place of "No key".
