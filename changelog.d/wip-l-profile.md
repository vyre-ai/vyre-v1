- Memory: each person has one profile per Space, read by all their agents, in two parts. Public is what teammates' chats may use: role, team, how to work with them, writing style, time zone and working hours. Private is everything else and stays in the person's own chats. Every turn loads the profile of the person who asked. Where anyone else reads the chat (a channel, a group chat, the Ask behind a record comment), it loads only the public part, plus the public part of the other people there. Where only the person and their own agents are present, it loads both parts. A fact one agent learns is known to all of that person's agents. New facts land in Private. A fact moves to Public only when the person moves it or says yes to an agent's "Add to your public profile?"
- Settings: a new Memory and privacy page. About you lists Public ("Teammates' chats can use this") and Private ("Only your own chats") with where each fact came from (a press opens the chat line). You can edit a fact in place, move it, add one, Forget it (with Undo, history keeps it), or Erase it completely after a confirm in the row. Erase removes the fact and every earlier version, and only the person it came from or an admin can do it. Who pays for memory moved here from AI.
- Chats: the Knows tab opens with About you, showing what agents in this chat may use about you and why: public and private in your own chats, public only where other people read.
- Erase completely now also removes every earlier version of a fact and the record of where recall used it.
- Settings: Who pays for memory no longer asks you to pick an account for your own memory. Learning uses the AI account you chat with, on its cheapest model, up to your daily limit, and the row shows today's model passes. The Space's and projects' accounts and the limits stay in the Console.
- Chats: when the people who read a chat change (someone joins, or a private chat becomes shared), the agent's engine starts a new session for it. A session that heard the private profile never answers once someone else is reading.
