- Saving to the Vault no longer waits for an unlock. A Mine item saved while your key is not on the Space (your outside agent working while the app is closed, a server that restarted, a sign-in that never handed the key over) is sealed to your own public key: the server can keep it and cannot open it. It opens the next time you are signed in, with nothing to press.
- An open app hands its key back to a Space that restarted behind it, on a Team Space server too, with no prompt. Where it has no key to give, the one password prompt opens the Vault in place on every Space reached over https, instead of "Sign out, sign in again".
- An outside agent with "Act for me, including setup" can now share Vault items with people, groups and roles, and set the Space AI key. Giving a key to an agent still stays with a person.
- Every Vault refusal says what happened and what to press. "Unlock it (sign in on the computer that runs this server)" and "No role of the caller includes the permission" are gone from the Vault, the Space AI key, connections, chats and automations; a guest, a member and an outside agent each get one plain line with what to do instead. The Ask tab makes your Mine folder when you have none, instead of "Vault has no folder to put this in".
- The Vault matrix: a live test of saving and using an item (in a chat, an automation and a connection) and sharing it, for every place a Space runs, every role, every place someone signs in from, every scope and every place a turn runs, with the provider checking it got the right key.
