- Saving to the Vault no longer waits for an unlock. A Mine item saved while your key is not on the Space (your outside agent working while the app is closed, a server that restarted, a sign-in that never handed the key over) is sealed to your own public key: the server can keep it and cannot open it. It opens the next time you are signed in, with nothing to press.
- An open app hands its key back to a Space that restarted behind it, on a Team Space server too, with no prompt. Where it has no key to give, the one password prompt opens the Vault in place on every Space reached over https, instead of "Sign out, sign in again".
- An outside agent with "Act for me, including setup" can now share Vault items with people, groups and roles, and set the Space AI key. Giving a key to an agent still stays with a person.
- Every Vault refusal says what happened and what to press. "Unlock it (sign in on the computer that runs this server)" and "No role of the caller includes the permission" are gone from the Vault, the Space AI key, connections, chats and automations; a guest, a member and an outside agent each get one plain line with what to do instead. The Ask tab makes your Mine folder when you have none, instead of "Vault has no folder to put this in".
- The Vault matrix: a live test of saving and using an item (in a chat, an automation and a connection) and sharing it, for every place a Space runs, every role, every place someone signs in from, every scope and every place a turn runs, with the provider checking it got the right key.
- Adding an AI key "Shared with the Space" no longer stops at "Choose the shared folder to keep it in". It goes in the Space's one shared folder you can add to, or else in "AI accounts", made the first time and shared with everyone in the Space. The sheet shows "In AI accounts" beside Belongs to, and you can pick another folder there. A second person's key for the same provider is named "OpenRouter 2" instead of failing as a duplicate.
- The Vault has four sections: AI accounts, Google and Microsoft, Connections, and Everything else (Keys, Logins, Cards), then the shared folders, Mine and Advanced. Each item shows in its one main section: a Google Workspace login is under Google and Microsoft, an OpenAI key under AI accounts. The + adds the kind of the page it is on; in All it first asks "An AI account, Google or Microsoft, an app or tool server, or a key, login or card". The key, login or card reel no longer mixes in AI providers.
- Google and Microsoft is its own page: two cards with their state and one button each, and their keys below. Connections lists the apps and tool servers your agents can use, with My connections and Space connections tabs in a Team Space and one list in a Personal Space. A Personal Space has no Mine row: everything there is yours.
- Monday and MCP servers have a mark of their own in the app's icon set (simple stand-ins until their artwork is supplied).
- AI accounts in a Team Space has two tabs, My AI accounts and Space AI accounts. A Space AI account row shows its mark, its name, whether it works and what it is used for ("Used for Automations · Goals · up to $500 a month"). Its sheet ticks Team chats (when someone has no AI account of their own, for everyone or chosen groups), Automations, Goals and Agents' own work (all agents or chosen ones), with a monthly cap, and saves with providers.uses.set. People's own AI accounts always come first in their own chats. Admins change them; everyone else sees them read-only. A Personal Space keeps one list.
