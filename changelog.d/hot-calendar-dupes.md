- Reconnecting a Google or Microsoft calendar no longer makes a second calendar and imports every event again: a connection for an account whose calendar is already here answers that calendar, and its pull reads through the new connection. Duplicates an earlier reconnect left are folded into the oldest calendar by the scheduled pull (each event once, the newest version wins, grants given again, one audit row), which also clears the "This event is not there any more" a duplicate copy gave.
