## What's new
- A Space admin can change who is on a project and in what role, even when they hold no Manage on that project. The role menu, Remove and Add People now work for them.
- The "Finish setup, 0 of 9" pill no longer shows in the top bar of a brand-new Space. Setup still opens on its own the first time. The pill appears once you finish or put off a step, counts the steps really done of the ones your device shows, and goes when setup is done.
- The leftover Home screen code is gone. Chats is the first screen. On the phone, You, My work now opens the Tasks list in Chats.
