- Asking the Assistant "do you have Fathom connected?" or to connect a service is now one card and at most a line of words before it and a line after. The card asks for what the service needs, tries the key first, then connects it and keeps it. A wrong key keeps nothing and marks the field; if the service cannot be reached nothing is kept and Retry uses what you typed. The same card is used again when the Assistant asks again, and a card you fold with Not now opens again with Connect.
- Where the key is kept is one quiet line, "Kept in Mine". The menu lists Mine first, then the shared folders an agent can use; folders an agent cannot use are listed under Restricted and cannot be chosen.
- A person who may not connect services in the Space is told so once and can ask an admin by name. The card then says when they were asked and finishes by itself when the admin connects it from their own card.
- In the Vault, an item kept where an agent cannot use it says so before you try: the share sheet lists the agents but cannot pick them and offers Move, the item page puts "Can't use this while it's Restricted" and Move under each such agent, and Move lists Restricted folders without letting you choose them.
- A connector built in one call has no health check, so a wrong key used to pass; its first read that needs no input now tries the key.
