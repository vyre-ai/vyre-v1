Describe a Flow in a chat and it shows up there as a card. When an agent drafts an Automation from a chat, the thread gets a card with the Flow's name, what it does in plain words ("When a record changes. Then make a task to call the lead. Then email me the summary, held for your yes") and two buttons, Turn on and Test, that run as you. The words are built from the definition, not written by the agent. Also fixed: Flows that start from an event (a record changes, an email arrives, a form is submitted) never started on a running server, because nothing delivered those events to them. They do now, and a Flow that makes the very record it listens for runs once and stops instead of looping.
