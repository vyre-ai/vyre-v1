Home opens with one line of what changed since you were last here, true without an assistant: "Since Tuesday: 2 tasks wait on you, 5 unread messages, 3 records you follow changed." Each part opens where it lives (Needs you, the Inbox, the activity). When nothing changed it reads "All quiet since Tuesday." and when nothing could be read (offline) it says nothing. Where you were last is kept on this device and moves up each time Home has been open for a few seconds.
