New record type in Records now names things properly. Typing Deals, Company or People gives the type "Deal", "Company", "Person" and the plural "Deals", "Companies", "People" (already-plural names stay plural, and the singular is worked out from a plural name). The Start with step offers email, phone, dates, amount, number, stage, status, choice, multi choice, checkbox, owner, files, URL, notes and rating as chips, with More to find the rest by typing, and picks the usual ones from the name (Deals get Amount, Stage, Owner, Close date and a link to Contacts). If a record cannot be made because its type is not ready on the device yet, you now see what to do instead of "no type deal". A live test makes types named Deals, Company, People, Matter and Status from the sheet's own calls, then creates, edits and lists a record in each.
