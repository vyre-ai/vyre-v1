- Your Personal Space can now be on more than one computer. On a new computer, choose Sign in and use your Vyre account: Vyre finds the Personal Space on your other computer through Vyre's relay and offers "Use your Personal Space from Office iMac". That computer shows the request in Settings, Computers, with a six-digit code to check against the one on the new computer. Allow it there, and the new computer opens your account with your password, copies the Space once and starts as the same Personal Space. The new computer no longer makes a second, empty Personal Space.
- The relay carries only sealed bytes for this: a hash of your username, a random secret, a sealed offer and the end-to-end channel between your computers. Your password never leaves the new computer. It opens the key bundle your other computer hands back, which is how the new computer knows it reached your own Space. A computer that answers for your username without your password's bundle gets nothing.
- Settings, Computers in a Personal Space lists Your computers: this computer, the others, whether each is awake, and when each was last seen. A new computer asking to use the Space shows there with Allow and Decline. Remove takes a computer out.
- Every computer of a Personal Space answers under the Space's one box key and keeps its own route at the relay, so a phone that pinned the key reaches whichever one answers.
