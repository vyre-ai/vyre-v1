- Your Personal Space can now be on more than one computer. On a new computer, choose Sign in and use your Vyre account: Vyre finds the Personal Space on your other computer through Vyre's relay and offers "Use your Personal Space from Office iMac". That computer shows the request in Settings, Computers, with a six-digit code to check against the one on the new computer. Allow it there, and the new computer opens your account with your password, copies the Space once and starts as the same Personal Space. The new computer no longer makes a second, empty Personal Space.
- The relay carries only sealed bytes for this: a hash of your username, a random secret, a sealed offer and the end-to-end channel between your computers. Your password never leaves the new computer. It opens the key bundle your other computer hands back, which is how the new computer knows it reached your own Space. A computer that answers for your username without your password's bundle gets nothing.
- Settings, Computers in a Personal Space lists Your computers: this computer, the others, whether each is awake, and when each was last seen. A new computer asking to use the Space shows there with Allow and Decline. Remove takes a computer out.
- Every computer of a Personal Space answers under the Space's one box key and keeps its own route at the relay, so a phone that pinned the key reaches whichever one answers.
- After a new computer joins your Personal Space it signs you in with the password you just typed. You are not asked for it again.
- The join also copies your agents' git repositories, so they work on the new computer as they did on the first.
- Every computer of a Personal Space keeps a full copy: chats, records, files and their contents, settings and your Vault (still locked by your password). Each one works with nothing else running. Your computers keep each other up to date through Vyre's relay whenever two of them are awake, end to end encrypted, with no ports to open. No computer is the main one: a change goes from whichever computer made it to every other one, through any that is awake.
- A computer that is offline keeps working on its own copy. What you change there waits and reaches your other computers when it can. The title bar says "Offline, N changes waiting" while none of them can be reached, and "Sending N changes" while they go. Settings, Computers shows each computer as awake or asleep, with when it was last seen.
- When two computers changed the same thing while apart, the later change wins on both. Different fields of the same item merge.
- Scheduled work runs on one of your computers at a time. Automations, routines, watchers, calendar and mail pulls and nightly jobs run on the computer that holds the Space's schedule. It renews its hold every 30 seconds. When it sleeps, another awake computer takes over after 90 seconds, and a server of the Space takes over from a computer. Each computer still looks after its own agents' files and its own running turns. Settings, Computers says which computer runs scheduled work.
- A scheduled run is keyed by its automation and its scheduled time. If two computers fired the same tick while they could not reach each other, they made the same Run, and syncing leaves one.
- A chat message that reaches a computer by sync starts nothing there: no turn, no mention and no watcher. The turn runs where the message was sent. The engine's queue and its running turns stay on their own computer.
- When two computers made something with the same name while apart, both are kept. The later one keeps the name and the other gets " (2)", the same on every computer. Anything a sync could not merge is kept whole, never dropped. Settings, Computers lists these under Sync conflicts.
- A scheduled automation sends, posts or pays only from the computer that holds the schedule at that moment. If another computer took over between the tick and the send, the step waits for that computer, which carries it on after the next sync. The step runs once, with the same key.
- Your phone reaches your Personal Space through whichever of your computers is awake. The computer that holds your phone address tells Vyre's relay about your other computers, and the relay sends the phone to the first one that is on. They all answer under the Space's one key, so the phone's link keeps working whichever one answers. When the computer a phone was using goes to sleep, the next page opens from another awake computer.
- The asleep page appears only when every computer is off. With more than one computer it says "Your computers are asleep" and lists each one with when it was last seen.
