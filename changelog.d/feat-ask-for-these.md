"Ask for these" on a record now takes you to the person, not to an assistant chat. It opens their conversation in the Inbox with the composer ready: an email (subject "A few details for your file") naming the fields the record still lacks, or a one-line text when the record has only a phone. A company or lead asks the contact it links to. In a Team Space with a public address the composer also adds a quick form, a one-use link whose answers fill the record; turn it off with the "Add a quick form" switch. Where a quick form cannot be made, one quiet line says "Quick forms need a Team Space". Nothing is sent until you press Send. A person with no conversation yet gets an empty one, and with no mailbox or number connected the Send from menu offers to connect one. A record with no email or phone opens its empty Email field instead.
