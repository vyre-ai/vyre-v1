Back and Forward now work in every tab. Each tab remembers where it has been: the arrows at the start of the title bar, Cmd+[ and Cmd+] (Alt+Left and Alt+Right on Windows and Linux), the mouse's back and forward buttons, a two-finger swipe in the desktop app and the browser's own Back all take the same step. Back from an agent's page returns to the chat you opened it from; a link opened cold goes up to its place. When a menu, sheet or dialog closes, focus goes back to the control that opened it.
