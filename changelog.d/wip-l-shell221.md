- Shell layout: one 8 px gutter between the window edge and every panel and between every pair of panels (rail, sidebar, main, side pane), in every place. The rail is one full-height panel with the lock, your face and the more button inside it. A layout model (ui/src/SidePane/layout.ts) decides which panels dock, fold or float by window width: 1280 and up all side by side, 1024 to 1279 an open pane folds the sidebar into the rail and it returns when the pane closes, 768 to 1023 the sidebar and pane open as sheets over main (scrim, Escape closes), under 768 one panel at a time. Main never drops under 480, a drag stops there, and widths are saved per window size band.
