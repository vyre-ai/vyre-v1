- Meetings close themselves: when a transcript lands, your own Assistant prepares a close-out with a short summary, the decisions and open questions, the action items as tasks for the right people (with due dates when said), updates and a meeting note for the attendees' records, a follow-up email to the outside attendees and a hold for the next meeting.
- The Inbox has a Meetings tab: each meeting shows Ready to close, Closed or Nothing to do. Open one to edit any item, approve it, skip it, or Approve all in one tap; Redo prepares it again without making anything twice.
- Approving goes through the usual capabilities and the Gate: tasks are created, records updated, the email sent (or saved as a draft if you prefer) and the hold added, and a closed meeting links to everything it made. You see your own meetings and those shared to a project or channel you can read.
- A task made from a meeting says "From" and the meeting, and a record's Everything tab lists its meetings.
- A meeting closes itself from the first one: when the transcript lands, the server finds the owner's Assistant (new agents.assistant_of, answered only for the server and the person themself) and the package is ready without anyone opening the Inbox.
