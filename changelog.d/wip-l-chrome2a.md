## What's new
- Vyre for Chrome: an agent working in your Chrome now gets its own tab group, "Vyre · <agent>", with its own colour. The bar sits at the bottom of the page with Take over and Stop, and says "Waiting for you in Vyre" while a send waits for you.
- Vyre for Chrome: the Vyre badge sits in sign-in and card fields. Click it to pick an account (or a card) from your Vault. Cards fill number, name, expiry and code.
- Vault, Connections: a Browser kind. For each site you choose where it runs (My Chrome, agent computers, or both) and what each agent may do there: Read only, or Act. Sends, posts, payments and deletes are always held for you. A site with no connection can be read by an agent in your Chrome and never acted on.
- Chats: an agent working in your Chrome leaves two cards. "Using your Chrome" shows a small masked picture of the tab with Watch and Take over, and "Show what <agent> saw" lists the Vault handles it was given by name. A held send shows To, From, Subject and Message with Send, Edit and Don't send. The cards are ready in the chat; what posts them waits for agents to be placed on your computer (see below).
- Vyre for Chrome: "Save to Vault?" keeps a new login in Mine by default. Choose Shared to pick a folder and the agents that may use it.

- Chrome: the extension asks for the tabGroups permission, and the held-send event now carries the To, From, Subject and Message read from the page (never a secret field), for the approval card in chat.
- Chrome: Take over gives the tab back and the agent waits until chrome_resume, like Stop.
- Chrome: Save to my agent no longer saves into Mine and then fails to share. A shared save goes to a folder the Space lists for the person, each save and grant carries a key made from the card so a retry does not duplicate, and a folder or agent the page names that the Space did not list is refused.
- Chrome tests: a real headless Chromium run of an agent in a tab group with a held send, Take over, Stop and Esc (apps/chrome/tests/ext-agent.tabs.e2e.test.ts).
- Browser connections capabilities: browser.sites.list, browser.sites.set and browser.sites.remove, a new module "browser" (lane L10; the frozen module count is now 75). The Chrome extension asks browser.sites.list and refuses an agent's click, typing or fill with a read_only error when the right is Read only, in My Chrome; an older app that cannot answer changes nothing.
- Chrome tests: the badge and picker, and Save then Shared with its keys, in a real headless Chromium (apps/chrome/tests/ext-core.signin.e2e.test.ts); Read only refusing an act and a Vault fill whose value never reaches the agent (ext-agent.tabs.e2e.test.ts).
- Chat cards: chrome_held and chrome_step blocks (apps/app/src/places/chats/conversation/chrome-cards.tsx) and lib/chrome-cards.ts, which makes them from the extension's held, frame, saw, driving and paused events. The extension adds the chat (thread) to those events when the caller of callTool names it, sends a masked JPEG every few seconds while an agent works in your Chrome, and forgets a held item when the app sends agent.held.drop (chromeBridge.dropHeld). Nothing in the daemon calls callTool for an agent turn yet, so nothing posts these cards: that is the placement of agents on the person's own computer.
- Skill: your-chrome (agent/skills/your-chrome/SKILL.md) tells an agent how to work in the person's Chrome: reuse its tab group, read before acting, Read only, what is always held, never type a password, Take over and Stop.
- Chrome fixes: the Save to Vault card was unreadable in a dark page (its fields kept a light background with light text); the agent bar on a phone-width page no longer cuts its words.
- An agent with no computer of its own can now work in its person's Chrome: the Chrome tools run there when the person signed in to that Chrome is the one the agent works for, in the Space they picked, after they open Chrome to agents (POST /local/chrome/agents). A held send and the first picture leave their cards in the chat.
- The agent bar and the held card now follow the approved prototype: dark 12px panel with the agent's initial, Esc key cap, frame in the agent's tab group colour; the held card has a Held band, Needs you, a Where row and "<agent> stopped before Send".
- Chrome tests: a page script cannot read the Save card, the picker or the password, even while Peek shows it.
- Chat cards from your Chrome now work from the card: Send releases through chrome_send once (a second press says it was already done), Don't send makes the extension forget it and tells the agent in the chat, Edit hands you the tab to change it yourself, Watch brings the agent's tab to the front, Take over gives it back. New capabilities browser.cards.post (a card posted with no text and no mention, so it never wakes an agent) and browser.chrome.act (only the person whose Chrome it is).
- The account picker shows the username under each login's name, so two logins for one site can be told apart. vault.items.match offers a username only when it is not a secret field, never a password.
- Chrome tests: a scripted chat turn drives the real extension in headless Chromium through the computers module and the browser cards (apps/chrome/tests/ext-chat-chrome.live.e2e.test.ts).
- Edit on a held-send card now makes To, Subject and Message editable in the card. Send then puts your edited values into the page before it presses Send, and what is sent is what you edited.
- A Team Space turn can work in your Chrome: the server asks your computer's daemon over your device link (chrome.available, chrome.call, chrome.drop, chrome.takeover, chrome.watch), which answers with the same port a local turn uses and only for the person the computer is linked as. Held sends and pictures come back as chrome_event frames.
- The agent bar now counts the plan ("Fill the form · 2 of 3"), has a hand icon on Take over, and shows Take over and Stopped states ("You're in control. <agent> waits." with Give back; "Stopped." with Resume and End task). Open in Vyre opens the chat the agent was asked in.
- The "Using your Chrome" card shows the plan's checklist, with "<action> waits for you" and the tab group line, above the held card.
- The Save to Vault card follows the prototype: header with a menu and close, the site, Keep in with a Mine and Shared segment and a one-line note, Not now and Save.
- Chrome tests: the booted daemon with the real extension and a scripted chat turn (host/tests/chrome-chat.live.test.ts) and the device-link questions (host/tests/device-link.chrome.test.ts).
- Chrome tests: a server-side turn for a member drives the extension on the member's own computer over the device link, with an edited send, and another person's turn cannot reach that Chrome (host/tests/chrome-team.live.test.ts, two booted daemons and the real extension). An error code the extension gives (held_gone) now survives the trip over the link.
- Vyre for Chrome: the Vyre badge now sits in every sign-in field at once (and the card number), the Save card has icons on Mine and Shared and an eye on Peek, and the chat's step card shows the tab's picture at full size.
- Chrome tests: the two-daemon test now also signs a second real member in on their own computer (real invite and accept, real device link): their turn is refused and the first member's page is not touched.
- Vyre for Chrome: the Save to Vault card shows the site's own icon beside its name (its initial where Chrome has none).
- Chrome tests that cross layers (the daemon, the computers module, the browser module and the extension in one test) moved to test/ (chrome-chat.live, chrome-team.live, chrome-chat.e2e.live); the Browser sheet lists its agents as ui/Row rows.
