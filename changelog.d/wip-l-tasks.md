- Your turn reaches you. When work is handed in for your review or approval, sent back to you, or given to you by someone else, it sits at the top of Tasks under Needs you and you get a notification that names the task and the person: "Maya handed in "Draft the letter" for your review". The Tasks count in the sidebar is exactly that list, each task once.
- Teams and roles can hold tasks. The assign menus list them. Everyone on the team sees a Take button, and the first person to take, start or hand in the task has it: they join its thread and the others see "Taken by Sara". Give it back from the task's menu.
- Agents start on any task they hold, including an Answer, Review, Approve or Unblock, and answer it with its own actions. If a task moves from one agent to another, the first agent stops. An outside agent (Claude Code, Codex) that sits on a task gets one reminder, and then the task goes to a person. Outside agents can read their task list with task_digest.
- Due dates use your time zone. "Due today" follows Settings > Profile > Time zone. Tasks reminds you when the day a task is due begins for you and again when the due time passes. A snoozed task comes back on its own, and you can find snoozed tasks before then under Show > Snoozed. If a review or approval sits for a day, the person holding it gets one reminder. Automations can start when a task is due.
- Tasks is the only place for what needs you. The separate Needs you page is gone, and its links open Tasks. When you come back after time away, "While you were away" sits at the top of Tasks.
- A task you press in a chat (a task card or a line of the plan) opens in the pane beside the chat. A task opened from the Tasks list has a way back to the list.
- The status menu shows only the moves you can make, in plain words: Start, Hand in for review, Send back, Approve. Marking open work Done hands it in, with its summary, from every door: the menu, the keys, the board.
- Names you can trust in Tasks: someone else's private agent shows as "Irfad Imtiaz (Agent)", teams and roles show their names, and attached files show their file names.
- The work breakdown updates when a child task moves. Search in Tasks finds words in the brief, notes and summary across all your tasks. Deleted tasks leave search, and restored ones come back.
- A task's source shows as "Part of <goal>" or "From <meeting>". Repeating tasks keep their brief, files and followers.
- Only someone on a task can edit its fields, people, links or dependencies. Anyone who can see the task can still follow it.
