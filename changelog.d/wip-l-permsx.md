- Console > Permissions: who may do what in this Space. The five built-in roles across (custom roles on request), the permission catalogue down by area; each cell says Allowed, Own only, Ask an admin or Not allowed, with a count where people differ. Own only is where an area has a part of a person's own and a part for the Space (automations, agents, sites, Vault, connections; lib/own-pairs.ts) and only the own part is allowed. Opening a cell lists them and offers Add an exception; a custom role's cell changes the role (preview, then apply). Preview as takes a role or a person and shows what they get, area by area.
- Per-person and group exceptions, on the access engine: Allowed, Own only (Allow on the own part plus Not allowed on the Space part, offered only where both exist) or Not allowed on a permission or a whole area, with an optional end and a reason. A person's own beats a group's, which beats the role; among peers Allow wins; the Owner is never restricted; an admin gives only what they hold; an end lapses on its own with an audit row; an agent acting for a person is refused what the person is refused and follows their Allow exceptions, within what an agent may ever hold. The gateway, identity.whoami's permissions and the app's gates read the same answer, and an open session picks up a change within seconds.
