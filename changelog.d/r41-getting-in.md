Getting into a Space no longer ends on a line with nothing to press. Opening Vyre with no Space open shows Your Spaces: every Space this device has used, with its picture and the person's name, one press (or a number key) to enter. A Space you signed out of stays in the list and opens its sign-in with your username already there. With no address named, the Space used last opens. Nothing in the list is a credential, and no saved sign-in is deleted by it.

One address box everywhere (Sign in, Add a Space, Join): it takes a bare name (northwind means northwind.vyre.run, shown under the box as you type), an address, an address copied from the browser while in the Space, an invite link, an owner link, or a relay Space's own link. A name with no Space says "No Space at ..." under the box. A pasted invite for another address now opens that invite, not that Space's sign-in.

The desktop app no longer answers "This Space is reached through Vyre's relay. Open the link in your browser." It opens a relay Space in the browser itself, with the link exactly as given, keeps it in Your Spaces (marked as opening in the browser), and opens it there again on a press. If that Space later moves to an address of its own, the same row signs in inside the app. A saved Space that moved to the relay is found there and opened in the browser, and its saved sign-in is kept.

Every way in asks again quietly before it says anything: the screen says Reconnecting, tries twice more, and only then names the Space ("Northwind is not answering") with Try again and the fixes that fit: Open in browser in the desktop app, Clear old data in a browser that holds an old worker or cached pages for the Space (the saved sign-in stays). A relay Space whose computer is off says it is asleep, when it was last seen, and checks again by itself. An invite or pairing link is said to have expired only when the Space answers; a Space that does not answer is said to be that. A worker left in a browser from before a Space moved between the relay and its own address is removed at the first failed call, not five seconds after the page loads. "I already have a Vyre account" tells a wrong name, a Space on the relay and a Space that is not answering apart.

For the relay (takes effect when the relay is next deployed): any page may ask what a vyre.run name is, so a wrong name and a sleeping Space read differently everywhere; and the relay's own page for a device with no key takes the invite or Space link in a box instead of saying to go and find it, with Try again where it could not start.

Getting in is one explicit state machine: from every screen there is a press that moves on and a path to a Space, and a saved sign-in is removed only by signing out, by the Space saying the session ended, or by clearing the browser's own storage. Being offline, slow, asleep, moved or open in two tabs never removes one.

A Space that opens but cannot load its pages now offers Your Spaces beside Try again. Your Spaces opens over the Space, which stays as it was: Back returns to the same screen, and the saved sign-in is never touched. The same extra way out is available to every error screen that needs one.
