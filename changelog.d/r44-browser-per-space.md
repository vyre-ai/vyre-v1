- Each Space now has its own browser container and volume (vyre-sbrowser-<space id>). The first time it starts, its agents' saved sign-ins are copied (never moved) from the old shared browser volume, only for profiles that provably belong to that Space (their folder names it, or it is a Personal Space, the only one on its computer). A failed copy changes nothing and is tried again at the next start; the old volume is left untouched, and the doctor line says how many profiles were left there. A purge removes the Space's own container, volume and network.
