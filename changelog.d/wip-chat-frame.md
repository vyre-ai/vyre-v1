- The "/" list in a chat now offers the whole command set under its headings (While it works, Bring in help, How it runs, This session, Keep it, Set it up) instead of 16 commands cut at eight. New: /review, /security-review, /agents, /effort, /new, /context (also /usage, /cost), /copy, /memory, /skills, /hooks, /init. "Continue in another Space" is gone from the list, as the chat design says.
- The chat's "..." menu is short and in sentence case: Plan first, Fork, Find in this chat, Export, Archive, and Stop everyone while an agent works. Rename is the title, and Star, Mute, Copy link and Delete are in the sidebar's chat menu.
- The chat head is quiet while an agent works (no green dot, no red stop; the stop is the composer's), and the people count includes the agents that are here.
- The composer's plus now opens a menu: Photo, File, From Files, Record, Link, Voice note, Add people and agents, and "Don't remember this chat". The unlabelled lock beside the mic and the purple dot on the settings icon are gone; the quiet line "Not remembering this chat" still shows while that switch is on.
- A message's menu is the short list in sentence case (Copy, Reply in thread, Make a task, Share to, Fork from here, Pin, Add reaction, then Details on an agent's reply). Copy link, Edit, Retry, Mark unread, Remember this and Delete are behind its last row, More. An agent's hover bar shows copy, thread, fork, pin and more; your own message in an agent chat shows copy, edit and more.
- The landing tab in the tab strip reads "New chat" instead of "Chats".
- The pane button opens the same pane in every chat, with the tabs of what the chat is: an agent or group chat reads Crew, Computer, Threads, Files, and more behind More; a channel reads Threads, Files, People; a chat with one other person opens on that person. The agent page (About) is no longer what the button opens; it opens from the agent's face. What was opened from the chat sits in the same row as the tabs.
- The "/" list is one line per command, as the prototype draws it: a small icon of its own, the name, and what it does in grey on the right; the group heads are sentence case. /usage and /context are two commands: /usage opens the usage card, /context opens the context card with the meters and Compact ("Keep this every time" included).
- The chat's pane has a Kept tab (decisions and kept notes, a press goes to the message) and the Crew tab ends with the session block (time, steps, context, plan). The head shows the chapter the chat is in, in grey after the title, and the "..." menu has Kept.
- With two or more agents working, the composer's stop offers "Stop <name>" for each and "Stop everyone".
- The chat's "..." menu ends with "More": Star, Mute, Add people, Copy link, and Leave or Delete, so a chat opened from search, a link or a notification (no sidebar row) still reaches them. Typing /us finds /usage; /status and /cost are found while typed and say "Same as /usage". New command /voice starts the mic. /agents, /memory, /skills and /hooks are offered only in a chat with an agent, and /agents lands on Threads when nobody else is working. A saved Notes tab keeps its title instead of reading "New chat".
- On a phone, a long press on a message opens its sheet with every entry of the menu in one list, the ones that are behind More on a desktop included (the sheet scrolls), for an agent's reply and for your own message.
- The phone sheet a long press opens is designed: no generic title, a one-line quote of the message, the frequent reactions on top, icon rows 44 px high in groups with hairlines, Edit or Retry and Delete always in view, Delete in red and asking once ("Delete this message?"). The stop choice opens above the composer, not over its text.
- A stopped reply says who stopped it: "You stopped this." for your own stop, "Stopped by Sara." for another person's (or agent's); a stop by the system, or one the server cannot name, keeps its old words. A place's own tab (Chats) is named Notes, Artifacts or Tasks while that screen is shown, and the landing's name returns when it is cleared, the same as a restored tab. A long press on a message never also opens the browser's own menu or text selection on a touch screen.
- Deleting a message from the desktop menu asks once, "Delete this message?" with Delete and Cancel (Return confirms, Escape cancels), as the phone sheet does.
