On a phone, a link to a view of a tab's place now opens that view. The phone shell used to drop the link's hash, so every Inbox link (the Unmatched list, Meetings, a filter) landed on the list of inboxes. The screen keeps the whole hash, the Inbox draws the view at its first frame, and the Unmatched list opens a conversation with one tap. Two phone walks cover the Meetings link and the Unmatched list, with a stranger's mail seeded through the API.
