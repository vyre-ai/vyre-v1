Each chat now runs in one of three modes, and the mode reaches the model. Ask first (the default) asks before an agent changes or sends anything. Auto lets it act on its own. Plan only reads and writes a plan until you approve it. In every mode, an email, text, post or payment is still held for your yes: Auto never skips that. You can also pick the model, how hard it thinks (low, medium, high, max) and which of your accounts pays, for one chat or as the agent's default, and an agent can move to your next account when one runs out of its limit (on unless you turn it off for that agent). Claude Code, Codex and Grok get the matching settings of their own; Codex has no plan mode, so Plan is read-only there. A headless CLI cannot stop to ask, so in Ask first its own file and shell tools wait until you switch the chat to Auto.
