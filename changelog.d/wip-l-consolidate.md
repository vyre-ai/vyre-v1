## What's new

- Machines now keep a history. Every computer that runs Vyre work reports its load every 30 seconds; the Space keeps a point a minute for a day and ten-minute points for 30 days.
- A machine has a nickname, limits on most turns and agent computers at once, Drain, and pinned agents. An admin sets them for the Space's machines; a member sets them for their own computers.
- "Room left" scores each machine from its CPU, memory, disk and turn limit. A turn goes to the first machine in the person's order with room, skips a busy one for the next, and the reply's "Ran on" says why it moved.
- Machine alerts (disk under 10 percent free, memory pressure, a machine offline with pinned work, a failed backup, an update waiting) show as a dot in the Console and as one Inbox line for each admin.
- On a member's own computer an admin sees its name, whether it is online, its capacity and Vyre's own work, and never its files or processes.
- The Console is nine places (Overview, People and access, AI, Agents and skills, Connections, Machines, Safety, Data, Space), each one page with quiet tabs. The 55 pages it grew from are tabs and text switches inside them, and an old address, a link or a search for an old name (Sessions, Infrastructure, Actors) lands on the new home.
- Roles, Permissions, Grants and the access checker are one Permissions tab with a text switch; the six Space defaults pages sit under the page they configure.
- Machines is one page for every computer that runs Vyre work: a health strip, alerts, one row each with what it runs and can run, and a page per machine with four hover charts over 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, health findings, limits, Drain and pinned agents.
- Add a server is one flow with one command and the fingerprint to compare. It opens from Console, Machines for a server the Space holds and from Settings, This device for a server only you use.
- Settings is seven pages: You, Your AI, Notifications, Appearance, Connected, This device and Privacy and security. This device shows your own computers and servers in full; Memory, Skills, Email and calendar and About moved into the pages that fit them.
- The Console shows machine alerts among what needs you, with a count on Machines.
