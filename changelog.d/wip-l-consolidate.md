## What's new

- Machines now keep a history. Every computer that runs Vyre work reports its load every 30 seconds; the Space keeps a point a minute for a day and ten-minute points for 30 days.
- A machine has a nickname, limits on most turns and agent computers at once, Drain, and pinned agents. An admin sets them for the Space's machines; a member sets them for their own computers.
- "Room left" scores each machine from its CPU, memory, disk and turn limit. A turn goes to the first machine in the person's order with room, skips a busy one for the next, and the reply's "Ran on" says why it moved.
- Machine alerts (disk under 10 percent free, memory pressure, a machine offline with pinned work, a failed backup, an update waiting) show as a dot in the Console and as one Inbox line for each admin.
- On a member's own computer an admin sees its name, whether it is online, its capacity and Vyre's own work, and never its files or processes.
- The Console is nine places (Overview, People and access, AI, Agents and skills, Connections, Machines, Safety, Data, Space), each one page with quiet tabs. The 55 pages it grew from are tabs and text switches inside them, and an old address, a link or a search for an old name (Sessions, Infrastructure, Actors) lands on the new home.
- Roles, Permissions, Grants and the access checker are one Permissions tab with a text switch; the six Space defaults pages sit under the page they configure.
- Machines is one page for every computer that runs Vyre work: a health strip, alerts, one row each with what it runs and can run, and a page per machine with four hover charts over 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, health findings, limits, Drain and pinned agents.
- Add a server is one flow with one command and the fingerprint to compare. It opens from Console, Machines for a server the Space holds and from Settings, This device for a server only you use.
- Settings is seven pages: You, Your AI, Notifications, Appearance, Connected, This device and Privacy and security. This device shows your own computers and servers in full; Memory, Skills, Email and calendar and About moved into the pages that fit them.
- The Console shows machine alerts among what needs you, with a count on Machines.
- An admin's agent list no longer includes another person's own agents, even with All in the Space on. A person's Assistant and the agents they keep private are never named to an admin: the Console shows one summary row per person with a count, the spend against their cap, a Spend cap and Pause all (which is audited and tells the person).
- Usage by agent folds agents you may not see into one People's own agents line.
- An admin who manages runs no longer sees a person's own agent runs (routines and agent runs) in the run lists or by id. They see a count per person instead (`runs.people_counts`), never the work. Shared on purpose, it still shows.
- The Console is ten places from the approved prototype: Home, People, Agents, AI, Data, Messaging, Apps, Computers, Security and Space. Each has a subtitle, a quiet tab strip and a "Needs you" strip first. Old page addresses still work and Cmd-K finds the old names.
- New Console pages: AI accounts (one switch, with a confirm that names who moves), What each provider may see, Voice (UI only for now), Requests, Activity, Connected apps, Servers, Agent computers, Transfer or delete, Export, Sending rules and Do-not-contact list.
- Home gains a Relay tile (version, and amber "Update available" when older than the Space server), This month and Quick actions.
- New read `providers.own_people`: who runs on their own AI sign-ins and how many each, without an account name or a value.
- Plain words in three permission labels and across the Console (Teams, Extra access, Approvals, Keep and delete, Labels). A copy test and a redirect test hold them.
- Deleting a Space is no longer immediate. `spaces.delete` schedules it 14 days out (typed legal name, Owner only, and an export from the last 24 hours or an explicit "Delete without an export"), every admin gets a "Needs you" line, an Inbox line and an email with the date, and `spaces.delete_cancel` undoes it with one click. A minute job removes the data only after the date. `spaces.get` shows `deletionAt`; new read `spaces.export_latest`.
- Console > Space > Transfer or delete: "Take an export first", "Delete without an export", and after scheduling "Scheduled for deletion on <date>. Everything is removed then." with a Cancel deletion button (the Owner and any admin see it).
- The sending limits work. The daily limit, sending window and speed (Space settings send.cap, send.window, send.throttle) are read by mail.send and telephony.send_sms: over the daily limit a send is refused with send_cap_reached and the time sending opens again; outside the window, or over the speed, an automation's or an agent's message is held (queued, not dropped) and says so in a new note on the answer. A person is not made to wait but still counts toward the limit. Sending rules no longer shows "Not adjustable yet".
- Do-not-contact: Add from a file takes a CSV (one column of emails or phone numbers, an optional reason column), says "Add 214 people to the do-not-contact list" before anything is sent, then adds them through contacts.suppression.import.
- A reusable Request to join link. An admin turns it on in Console, People, Requests (new `members.join_link.create`, `.rotate`, `.disable`, `.get`), picks the role people join as (Guest by default), copies the address any time and rotates it to end the old one. A person who opens it asks with a name and an email (`members.join_link.request`, `members.join_link.preview`), nobody joins without an admin, and every admin gets an "asked to join" line in the Inbox.
- Approve on a join request (`members.join_requests.approve`) makes a one-time invite for their email at the link's role and shows it once to copy or email; Decline (`members.join_requests.decline`) closes it, and `members.join_requests.list` lists them. The Requests page lists them with Approve and Decline and counts them in the line at the top.
- Console, AI, What each provider may see: an admin marks each shared AI account "This account keeps nothing" (new `providers.set_keeps_nothing`, audited, off by default, always on for a local model). Restricted is open to a provider only while one of its accounts keeps nothing, and a Restricted chat runs only on such an account.
- feat(console): Computers has a CI runners tab (off state and Turn on sheet, month cost against the cap, runs, run sheet, cleanup log, paused at the cap, every setting calling ci.settings.set) and Apps has a GitHub tab (own GitHub app, values in Vault, Create one for us, the Space installation, new permissions line); wip/l-ci-runners and wip/l-install merged in, the ten-place Console kept.
- merge: right pane keeps About and gains the Skills tab; notification kinds item.shared and ci.paused join the Space deletion kinds; module count 78; audit wording (lib/words/audit.ts) in the Console.
- Console > Agents > Skills and plugins opens with "Installed for the Space": each install with its kind, who installed it, source and commit, who it is for, Update (preview first) and Remove (names who loses it), and Install from a link (read, then install).
- Space > General now carries Plan and usage: the design-beta sentence, AI spend this month against the Space limit (with Spend and limits) and storage. An old Billing address lands on General.
- Console > Agents > Hooks: Needs you now reads the Space's blocked, failed and timed-out hook runs of the last 7 days straight from the server, so an admin's strip never misses one. `hooks.runs.list` takes `since`, `outcomes` and `scope: "space"` (an admin reads every Space hook's runs; anyone else gets only what they could see anyway).
- Hooks rows have dividers, wrap in full and put Pause and Remove under the text on a phone. Remove is a quiet button, red only on the confirm, and a Removed note offers Undo. The Add a hook sheet says When, Do this, Only for and Stop after.
- Console > Apps > GitHub: a Needs you row at the top asks the admin to accept new GitHub permissions, with an Accept button.
- Console > Apps > Connected apps: a custom app you started stays on the list while it is a draft, with a Settings button, so you can come back to finish and publish it. Removing an app from its Settings returns to the list.
- Console > Computers: an added server's page ends with "Remove this server…". It asks first, says its agent computers stop, their local data is wiped and its access ends at once, then removes it. The Space server has no Remove.
- An agent made for a project is now one of the project's people in the same write, with the role Member. It is listed with the project's people and a Lead can make it Lead or Reviewer there; if adding it fails, the agent is not left behind.
