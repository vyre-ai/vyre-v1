- Records can be shared like Files. A record type and a single record open the same Share sheet: who can see it (Everyone in the Space, Only people added, Only me), a level per person (Can view, Can edit, Full access) and two switches, Can export and Can share with others. A grant can carry the export right, so a person can export a type without a role change; a type also says who may export (Admins only, Anyone who can view, Chosen people). One record can follow its type or be set apart ("this record only") and put back. The Records list can show only what was shared with you, with a small mark on rows that differ from their type. A record cut off from its type stays out of reach of every grant on the type, for people, agents and search alike.
- A record type someone was not given is gone for them everywhere: not in the sidebar, the type pickers, search, the Schema map, the change history, or as the target of a relation from a type they can see; a relation value into a record they cannot see is dropped, and the relations map leaves such records out. Records Export now works: it writes a CSV into the person's own Exports folder in Files, with only the rows they may read.
