The record page's right pane works. Activity is the record's real history from the log, newest first: who created it, who changed which field (the old and new value are in the tooltip), stage moves, links, Trash and sync pushes, one compact row each with a face. Comments are threads on the record: write, reply, edit your own, resolve and reopen, with a Resolved filter that brings hidden threads back. Typing @ offers people and agents with their faces. A person you mention gets an Answer request in Needs you that names the record and the comment. An agent you mention answers under the comment on its own, headless, with "Intake is replying" shown while it works; no chat appears in Recent. Chat is the record's own Ask pane, drawn in the tab. Files lists the record's folder (a project's own, or "Record files" made on the first attach), takes Attach and dropped files, and a click opens the viewer. Every tab has a short empty state. New: records.activity and records.comments.list, add, edit, resolve and delete.
