## What's new
- In a project chat or a channel, an agent you ask now answers in a thread under your message. The chat keeps your question and one live line under it.
- The line under your message shows the agent working with a timer, how many replies there are and when the last one came. Press it to read the thread.
- On a wide screen the thread opens in the side pane. On a phone it opens as a full-screen sheet with a back arrow.
- Approvals still come to the chat as one quiet line, and the agent can mark an answer "Also sent to the chat". You can tick "Also post to the chat" on your own reply too.
- A reply that also went to the chat says so in the thread: "Also sent to the chat".
- A new switch, "Agents reply in threads", sits at the foot of the thread and in the chat menu. It is on by default in projects and channels and off in direct chats and with your Assistant.
- A question you ask inside a thread is answered in that thread.
