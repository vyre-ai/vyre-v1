- The Space's Google Workspace connection no longer reads "Needs sign-in, never used" while it works: delegation setup marks it active after its check passes, and every good answer from Google (mail or calendar poll) stamps its last use at most once a minute. A connection stuck pending is repaired by the next poll. New system capability connections.mark_working.
- A server restart no longer leaves a signed-in person on the Space key for good: while the app is open, a Space server that says it holds no key (and no key in the app) shows the launch unlock once (Not now is remembered until the server holds the key again), every unlock through the Space door writes the server's wrap, and a locked key reads "Your sign-ins are locked on this server. Unlock Vyre to use them." in the chat and in errors, never as a sign-out.
- The lock that comes back while Vyre is open (the server restarted and lost the key) says why in one quiet line, and a live test follows the desktop path: sign in, unlock at launch writes the server's wrap, restart, relaunch locks, one unlock brings the person's own Claude back.
- Mid-session, after a server restart, the app stays on screen: a slim banner says the sign-ins are locked, Unlock opens a small sheet over the open screen (password, Unlock, Forgot password) and the x does what Not now does; the full-screen lock is for launch only. The one chat line when a turn fell back to the Space key carries the same Unlock.
- A brand icon that fails to load (GoHighLevel and every tool icon) falls back to the lettered tile instead of a broken image. The Vault item's "Who can use it" lists people and agents with no "Person" or "Agent" line; only what a name alone does not say is written (Group, Role, Automation, Outside agent, and how it came to hold the item).
- The top bar has one notifications slot right of the search field: the lock ("Sign-ins locked", Unlock, no dismiss), the connection (offline, sending), and what used to be toasts (shown briefly, with Undo when it can be taken back) appear there as one quiet line with its action, "+N" for more, and a small popover with all of them and the recent ones; on a phone it is a bell with a dot. Cmd-L (Ctrl-L elsewhere) locks Vyre, clearing the key from the app and from the server for that person, and opens the password sheet while locked; it is also Cmd-K "Lock Vyre" and "Unlock" and is in the keyboard shortcuts list. The mid-session lock is no longer a banner or a full-screen screen.
- Replies, mentions, held approvals, machine alerts and a ready update now reach the person in the top bar's notifications slot as notices that stay until handled, each with one action (Open, Review or Restart). The separate banner stack that used to pop up over the screen is gone.
- On a phone, a slim row with the notifications bell and its dot sits above the screen while something needs you, and takes no room otherwise. Work handed in for your approval says Review.
- Fixed the shell failing to open ("Couldn't open Vyre") after the banner stack was removed; found by opening the real app, not a mount.
- Opening Chats, a project page or Settings in a Team Space no longer makes the server refuse a call and print an error in the console: the list of imported local sessions answers empty, with a note that local history lives in the Personal Space. The Personal Space is unchanged.
- A first-hour walk (scripts/shots/first-hour.mjs) drives a real booted install through the real app, step by step, one paper shot each, and fails on any refused call or console error; steps it cannot drive yet fail by name instead of passing.
- A server that was not installed with vyre-server (run from source, updated by its host) now answers Server updates with "updated outside Vyre" instead of a refusal, so the Updates page loads without an error.
- Saving a mailbox password for Other email (IMAP) in your own Vault now works: the server reads it as you, not as the Space, so connecting no longer fails with "did not accept the sign-in" before it reaches the mailbox. When the Vault is locked or has no such item, it says so.
- The first-hour walk can run the crew and held-email steps through a scripted model that calls real tools (scripts/shots/first-hour-scripted.mjs).
- A member's own mail or calendar read through the Space's Google delegation no longer tries to tell the connection it works (a call the member may not make, refused on every read): only the host's polls and checks, or someone who manages the Space connection, do.
