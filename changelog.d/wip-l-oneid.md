- One Vyre account across your Spaces. When the desktop app already has your account, an owner claim link or an invite opens on "Continue as <username>" and asks only for your current password. The new Space gets the account you have: the same password and the same recovery code, with no new code to keep. The Space is added to the switcher and opens. "Use a different account" leads to the full form.
- With no account on the device (a phone or a fresh browser), the claim and join forms say they create your Vyre account, used for all your Spaces.
- A claim link opened in a desktop browser offers "Open in Vyre", which hands the link to the app (vyre://claim). Pasting a claim link into the app now sets the Space up inside the app instead of leaving it for the web page.
- Settings, Sign-in: "Make a new recovery code". You enter your password and get a new code, shown once with Copy and Save. The old code stops working in this Space and in every other Space on this device that has the same account. Any Space it could not reach is named.
