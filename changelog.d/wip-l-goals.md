- Goals: give an agent an outcome with a finish line. A goal reads its number from a real source (a records count, a connection, an outside tool, an automation, a number the owner keeps, a checklist, or the owner saying done), plans the work as tasks under one parent, wakes its lead agent in the goal's own chat at the check-in rhythm, when a task finishes, when the number moves and when an answer comes back, and runs on the owner's account. It goes Stuck with the reason after several flat check-ins and tells the owner once, pauses at its budget after a warning at 80%, respects quiet hours, ends Done or Missed with an honest summary and an offer to extend, and keeps a retrospective in its folder and in memory. Steps carry ids so a retried wake-up never repeats a send. Standing permissions the owner presses for hold only in the goal's chats while it runs.
- Every goal has a Files folder under its project (or under the owner's files), shared with the owner, the lead agent and the helpers; people mentioned in the goal chat can read it.
- Agents get the goals tools (goals_get, goals_progress_read, goals_plan_update, goals_checkin_post, goals_ask, goals_mark_stuck, goals_complete, goals_search_past, goals_sources, goals_step, goals_folder) and the working-on-goals skill.
