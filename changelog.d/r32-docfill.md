A filled document can now be as large as 25 MB, so a template with a letterhead picture fills without an error. Money prints as an amount in its currency ($1,250.00), an address on one line, a checkbox as Yes or No, a list as words joined by commas, a date and time and a duration as words, and a file or record link as its name. A sealed value, or a link that cannot be named, now stops the fill with a plain sentence naming the field instead of printing [object Object].
