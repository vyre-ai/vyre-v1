Making a channel is now easy to find and quick to do. A Team Space always shows a Channels heading, with a "Create a channel" row when there are none, so the first channel can be made. The plus at the top of the sidebar is a new menu (New chat, New channel, Browse channels), the Channels heading has a plus and a Browse button, and Cmd-K has New channel. The New channel sheet takes a name with a live #slug under it, an optional topic, Public or Invite only (hover for the difference), one search for adding people and agents with their faces, and an optional project link. Enter creates and opens the channel, Esc closes, a taken name shows under the name field, and a refusal shows in the sheet. Browse channels lists the public channels you are not in, each with Join. The sidebar list now shows the channels you are in. A Personal Space has no channels, so none of this shows there. On a phone the sheet fills the screen. Section tools in the Chats sidebar now have hover tooltips.
