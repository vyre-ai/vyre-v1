A computed lookup or rollup is judged on the records it was computed from, not on the record's links as they are by the time it is read, so an unseen link removed a moment ago does not reveal its amount. A link to a record in Trash makes the value empty for everyone until it is recomputed. One edit of a record that many others look up recomputes each of them, one by one, without merging repeated edits. A past version of a record shows no computed values. A fill that is stopped goes on from where it stopped.
