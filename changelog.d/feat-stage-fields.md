Record types can group their fields, and a Journey can attach fields to each stage and ask for some to be filled before a record changes stage. In the Fields panel, New group, rename, delete (its fields return to the ungrouped ones) and Move to group put fields into named groups; on the record page each group is a section, and a type with three or more groups shows them as tabs under the title, Overview first. In the Journey designer a stage has Fields for this stage, Must be filled to move here and Must be filled to move on, each picking an existing field or making a new one. A Journey record's page leads with the group of its current stage, lit; earlier stages show their fields; later stages stay locked with "Opens at <stage>". The Journeys module refuses a stage change while the required fields are empty, for people, Automations and agents alike, and lists what is missing; on the board and the stage stepper that refusal opens a small sheet where the person fills the fields and the move completes in one step.
