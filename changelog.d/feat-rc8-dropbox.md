Dropbox as a file source. Mount a Dropbox folder, or all of Dropbox, like Google Drive or SharePoint: the folder stays the source of truth and Vyre reads its change feed, so a pass after the first reads only what changed. A person connects their own Dropbox (OAuth with PKCE, the refresh token kept in their Mine vault), or an admin connects one for the Space (kept Shared). The Dropbox app key and secret come from the Vault item #Dropbox_App, never from the code. Writes can name the revision they were based on and land only on it; a file that moved on answers a version conflict and is never overwritten. Dropbox change notices arrive at /hooks/dropbox, verified with the app secret, and its address check is answered. Dropbox is a new source in Connect a folder, with its real mark, and Connect Dropbox opens in place over the sheet.
