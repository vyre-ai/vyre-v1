- Chats: your agent can message a teammate for you. Ask "tell Andrey the deck is ready" and it goes in your own direct chat with Andrey, signed "<You> (Agent)" with your face. Andrey's reply comes back to the chat you asked in and the agent carries on there. If you did not ask for it this turn, you get a "Send this to Andrey?" card first; one tap sends it, and so does a plain "yes, send it" in the chat. The card then shows "Sent to Andrey · approved in chat".
- Chats: your agent sees who is in. For each teammate it sees online, away or offline, when they were last seen, their local time and working hours, and when a message would reach them. It never sees anything private.
- Chats: a message to a teammate waits while they are away or outside their working hours, unless it is urgent. You can also send it when they are next online or at a set time. While it waits, you see "Scheduled for Andrey" in your chat.
- Chats: at most 3 agent messages an hour go from one person's agents to one teammate. More wait for the hour, and you are told.
- Chats: a teammate can mute agent messages from you for today, from the message menu or the quiet line under the newest one (shown once). What your agents send waits until tomorrow, and you are told.
- Chats: your agent can send a teammate a card: an approval, a review, or a request to share a key. The message is the card alone, so nothing is said twice. They answer it in place, and the answer goes back to your agent.
- Privacy: before a message goes, Vyre checks it against your private profile facts and your Mine Vault values. If one is in it, the message does not go, unless you typed that detail yourself.
- Chats: a direct chat is named for the other person and shows their face, instead of "Chat".
