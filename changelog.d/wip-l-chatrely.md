- Chat: a person's own connected account now comes before any Space-provided account (OpenCode Go and others) when a chat picks its default account, in the account picker and in the resolver (provider spelling `opencode_go` and `opencode-go` no longer passes the own account over).
- Chat: the model, provider and effort a person picked last now start every new chat (first screen, thread, Ask), across all agents and devices; it is no longer limited to the agent they picked it for.
- Vault: signed in means unlocked. The person key opened by the password at sign-in is sealed on the device (non-extractable WebCrypto key in IndexedDB), restored on every launch and handed to the node again after a restart, so no lock screen, unlock sheet, "Sign-ins locked" notice or Cmd-L is needed. Sign-out and ended sessions remove it. A session with no key on the device is signed out instead of locked. The "cannot read credential" messages now say the Vault key is not on this server.
- Chat: a chat with no pick of its own and Ask show the last model pick before the first turn; Auto counts as a pick. "Lock Vyre" is gone from the account menu and the phone. A session with no Vault key on the device is signed out once with "Sign in once more so Vyre can keep your Vault ready on this device."
- Models: a model check that fails because the Vault key is not on the node now ends once for the whole account and reads "Not checked"; a check that takes over 10 s reads "Not checked"; a model being checked or not checked can be picked. A Vault read that fails for a missing key tries to bring the key back once and retries; any other read failure names the sign-in and what failed instead of "The Vault could not be read".
