- Match an address to a person. New capabilities google.dwd.map_member and ms.consent.map_member (admin only, Gate-held for agents and automations): the owner, or anyone whose work address differs from their invitation, is matched by hand, and their mail and calendar then come in. An address belongs to one person and a person to one address; a clash is refused until the first match is taken away.
- The Google and Microsoft pages list people with a Match to... section (address plus person) and an Unmatch button on each matched person.
- Bring a team in from the directory. New google.directory.list and ms.directory.list (admin only) show the Workspace or tenant people and who is already in the Space; google.directory.import and ms.directory.import make one invitation each for the people an admin chose, as links that are only sent after a person approves; google.dwd.sync_members and ms.consent.sync_members match the people who have joined since to their address and connect their mail and calendar. Google delegation now asks for one more scope, admin.directory.user.readonly, so existing admins add it in Google Admin. The Google and Microsoft pages have a Bring your team in section.
