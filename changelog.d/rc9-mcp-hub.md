Connect an app hub. In Console, Plugins, one card connects Composio or Pipedream with one key. The key goes to your Vault and the Space keeps only its name. The hub's apps then show up as tools for every agent, and each card and tool row says "Runs through Composio". Each app has a Connect button to the hub's own page, and the list refreshes when you come back. Reads run on their own; a change asks first when an agent makes it, and a tool the hub does not mark as read-only counts as a change. Mail, files, CRM and the Vault never go through a hub. Remote tool servers that sign in with their provider instead of a key now work too: pick Sign in when you add one, finish in the browser, and Vyre keeps the token sealed and renews it on its own.
