- Google and Microsoft pushes now check who sent them. `/hooks/google/<kind>` takes a Gmail or Meet Pub/Sub push or a Calendar or Drive channel notice only with the Space's push secret; `/hooks/microsoft` takes a Graph notice only when its subscription is ours and its client state matches, and answers a new subscription's validation handshake. Before this, `google.receive_push` and `ms.receive_notification` could be called by any signed-in person or agent, and the Graph one trusted the caller's own "client state is fine" flag. Both now refuse a direct call. Calendar and Drive watches must use this server's own `/hooks/google/<kind>` address.
