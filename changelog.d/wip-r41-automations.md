- Every Automation says what it does in one plain sentence, written from its steps: under its name in the list and on top of its page ("When a customer's stage becomes Signed, draft an email and create a task.").
- What an Automation is missing shows before anyone turns it on: no AI account, no mailbox, an app that is not connected, a file that is gone or a step that is not finished, as one short line with one Connect or Fix button in the list, on the first page and on its page.
- Test is a dry run on something real: pick a record or a thing that happened last week, watch each step run, and read what was held instead of sent. It also says how many times the Automation would have started last week.
- "Why didn't this run?" on a record lists every Automation that watches that kind of record and what became of each: did not start (and the check that stopped it), stopped or failed at a step, waiting, ran, or off.
- A signature request now waits for a yes unless its author turned "Ask before sending" off on that step; before, it went out like an email once the Automation was on.
- "Ask before sending" on a chat post now really waits for approval; before, the switch did nothing for a post.
- A Journey can be a pipeline on a record type: the records of the type move through its stage field themselves, with no separate Journey record, channel or folder per record. Every move still goes through the Journey, so its gates, tasks, Automations and time limits cannot be skipped. A Journey made the old way moves onto its subject type with `journeys.to_pipeline`; a stop halfway resumes, nothing is deleted, and the old records keep their channels and folders.
- A board card shows the type's card face (or a person's own): its fields in the order chosen, and a cover for the visual face.
- A Journey opens on a Path: its stages as columns with the Automations that run in each, how a record gets in, where it ends, what travels with it, where one record is on it (how long in its stage against the limit) and a test of the whole path that tries every stage's Automations on one record without sending anything.
- A record type has a card face: a preset (simple, detail or visual) and up to six fields, set with `type.update` (the same write for a person's editor, an agent and the API) and checked against the type's own fields. System types may choose their card.
- After the upgrade the Journeys made the old way move onto their subject type by themselves, in the background, one a minute. A Journey that cannot move keeps the reason and waits for Retry; nothing is deleted and the old records keep their channels and folders.
- A Journey carries values from stage to stage for each record: the steps after read them as {{journey.plan}}, the "Remember for the Journey" step keeps one, a name no Journey carries is refused when the Automation is saved, and a value nothing has kept yet stops the step in words instead of sending a blank.
- The three SyncSpec tools show the spec once to a model (the first tool carries the shape, the others point at it); the server still checks every field. This frees about 10,000 characters of the tool-size budget.
- An AI step can be given the Space's skills ("Skills" on the step): their instructions go into its brief, in an Answer step and in a Task, so "draft the welcome email with the intake skill" works inside an Automation. The save refuses a skill the Space does not have and names the ones it has; a skill that goes missing stops the step with its name.
