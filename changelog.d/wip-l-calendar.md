## What's new
- The calendar week view is easier to read. Events that overlap now sit side by side in columns of equal width, and their titles wrap to two lines before they are cut.
- A long "Block" or "Focus" event is drawn as a quiet tinted band behind the day instead of taking a whole column.
- The all-day lane shows one chip per event per day, a multi-day event as one bar across its days, and a "+2" when the lane is crowded.
- The grid has a faint line every 15 minutes, today is tinted, weekends are quieter, a time pill sits on the current time line, and the view opens at the current time.
- Rest the pointer on an event to see a small card with its title, time, who is coming and a Join button. Dragging to make an event shows the time range as you go.
- Month shows up to three events a day and "+N more". On a phone the calendar opens on the day view, swipes between days and lists the all-day items at the top.
