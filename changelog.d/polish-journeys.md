New Journey type is made from real record types: pick who goes through it by name (Contacts, Companies, Projects) or add a new plain type in place, edit the stages as a list (add, rename, drag or arrow to reorder, delete) or start from a Sales, Delivery or Hiring template, see the board columns as you go, and read any problem in plain words next to the field. A Journey's type shows in Records under Journeys and opens as a board: drag a card to move its stage, press the plus in a column to start a record there, and the table stays one click away. A Journey record's page leads with a stage stepper and the subject as a link. Automations > Journeys lists each Journey with its stages and records, and Open board in a Journey now opens that board.
