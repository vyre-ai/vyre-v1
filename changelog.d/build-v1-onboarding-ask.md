- The onboarding screen's Assistant is live once the person has an AI account: the middle column shows their own Ask thread with their private assistant (one thread per person and flow, about the onboarding screen), with the card above it. Each message carries the screen context (the step, every step's state, the focus skill for the flow) through the Ask door, so the assistant knows where the person is. Before an AI account exists, the pre-written words stay.
- The `focus-onboarding`, `focus-onboarding-team` and `focus-onboarding-member` skills use real tool names (`memory_propose`, `spaces_onboarding_mark`, `kits_list`).
- Onboarding screen polish: the right column shows the real state a step has set up (the connected AI account as a chip "in your Vault", the facts saved from the interview, paired devices, the team and open invites, the Kit and its agents, folders), or "Not yet" with a one-line hint. The bottom bar is Skip for now (marks the step Later and moves on), Next (Done on the last step) and a quiet Finish later link that leaves for Home. Team AI shows the same-account offer alone first and the sign-in tiles only after "No, add a different one". The live Assistant is a compact conversation (messages and composer only, filling the column), via a `compact` option on the chat conversation.
- The team step "Your team" lists the connected tenant's people (`google.directory.list`, `ms.directory.list`), offers those not suspended and not already in the Space, and brings the ticked ones in with `google.directory.import` / `ms.directory.import` as Members. Each gets an invite link, shown once; nothing is sent.
- After people are brought in from the directory, the card drafts one email per person (their invite link, who invited them, one line on what Vyre is), lists the drafts, and one press sends them from the owner's own mailbox through `mail.send`, so the Space's send rules (caps, windows, suppression, the Gate) apply to each; a held or failed send is reported per person. The links stay on screen as the fallback, and with no mailbox connected the card says so. The directory reads one page at a time, with "Show more people" using each source's own token.
- The "Sign in for your domain" step reads the Space's providers (`members.sso.list`), says for each whether it is on and for which domain, warns when one is on without the domain (it would sign nobody in), and can run `members.sso.test`. Setup stays in the Console (`members.sso.set`); only existing members and accepted invites can sign in.
