- Crew: a teammate's thread always runs on the host (placement reason `crew`), because its repository copy is there; a Personal Space says in the Crew tab that its crew "Runs while this computer is on". The lead can wait on the checks of a pull request (`engine.crew.wait`): the host looks every 15 seconds, the Crew tab shows "Waiting · 12 of 40 done", and when they end one message wakes the lead. A merged card offers Push, which sends the base branch to the remote from the host and holds at the Gate for approval (`engine.crew.push`).
