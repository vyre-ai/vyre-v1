- "I already have a Vyre account" on every invite and owner claim. On a phone, a fresh browser or a new computer, enter the address of a Space you are already in and sign in there with your username and password. The new Space gets your Vyre account: the same password and the same recovery code. The sign-in at the other Space ends at once, and this device keeps nothing there.
- If that Space signs you in with Google or Microsoft, or your password is gone, use your recovery code instead. You choose a password once, and it signs you in to the new Space. Your recovery code stays the same everywhere.
- If this device already knows you from a Google, Microsoft or GitHub sign-in, an invite or claim link now opens on "Continue as <name>". It asks once for "Your Vyre password (the one you set when you joined), or your recovery code". After that the device remembers your account, and the next Space only asks for your password.
- Settings, Sign-in: "Use one account everywhere", for people who made two Vyre accounts before one account worked across Spaces. Pick the account to keep, then enter its password and the other account's password or recovery code. The other Space then signs you in with the kept username, password and recovery code. Your work, private items, role and place in that Space stay as they were. Other devices signed in there sign in again, and the change is in the audit log.
- The sign-in words say "your Vyre account" everywhere. A form only says it creates an account when it does.
