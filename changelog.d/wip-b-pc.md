- An agent's Computer tab is a list of its terminals and one picture of its browser. There is no Attach button: the agent's terminal is already open when you look, and the terminals of its helpers and the people working in it are rows too, each with what it last printed. Press a row to open that terminal full screen.
- Press the browser picture in the Computer tab to open the browser full screen, live. Take over and Give back are there, not in the tab. A sleeping browser wakes when you press its picture. Closing the browser while you hold it hands it back.
- In the full-screen terminal you watch until you take the keyboard with one press (the button, or Return). While you hold it the agent's commands wait and Esc goes to the terminal; Give back, or closing the window, returns it. A hold nobody uses for 15 minutes ends by itself. Taking the keyboard from another person needs the terminal's owner or an admin.
- The Computer tab lost its "Asleep" chip, its explanation lines, its Steps list and the paragraph about the older desktop. Each state is a dot and one word. Signed-in sites show only when there are some.
- A person's own terminal tab is named for them: "You" and "Your tab" for yourself, their name for everyone else. The full-screen terminal has one bar. Keys typed right after pressing Take over are no longer lost.
- While a person holds a terminal, the agent's commands wait in every tab of its computer, and it cannot close, rename or resize the held one. The keyboard stays yours while you read or watch, and ends by itself after 15 quiet minutes without anything writing or announcing it.
