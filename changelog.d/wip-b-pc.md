- An agent's Computer tab is a list of its terminals and one picture of its browser. There is no Attach button: the agent's terminal is already open when you look, and the terminals of its helpers and the people working in it are rows too, each with what it last printed. Press a row to open that terminal full screen.
- Press the browser picture in the Computer tab to open the browser full screen, live. Take over and Give back are there, not in the tab. A sleeping browser wakes when you press its picture. Closing the browser while you hold it hands it back.
- In the full-screen terminal you watch until you take the keyboard with one press (the button, or Return). While you hold it the agent's commands wait and Esc goes to the terminal; Give back, or closing the window, returns it. A hold nobody uses for 15 minutes ends by itself. Taking the keyboard from another person needs the terminal's owner or an admin.
- The Computer tab lost its "Asleep" chip, its explanation lines, its Steps list and the paragraph about the older desktop. Each state is a dot and one word. Signed-in sites show only when there are some.
- A person's own terminal tab is named for them: "You" and "Your tab" for yourself, their name for everyone else. The full-screen terminal has one bar. Keys typed right after pressing Take over are no longer lost.
- While a person holds a terminal, the agent's commands wait in every tab of its computer, and it cannot close, rename or resize the held one. The keyboard stays yours while you read or watch, and ends by itself after 15 quiet minutes without anything writing or announcing it.
- Typing in the full-screen terminal shows at once: the keys and the screen they made come back in one answer, and keys that did not land are sent again in order instead of being dropped.
- While a person has taken over the browser, only they see its picture; everyone else sees who is using it. The browser picture in the Computer tab no longer keeps a sleeping browser awake.
- The full-screen terminal and the Computer tab say what is wrong in each fault, with its one action: a terminal that ended (New tab), a connection that dropped (Reconnecting, the last lines stay), a computer starting, a browser that stopped (Restart).
- Text typed into a terminal before a program has turned its echo off is echoed, and programs that clear typed-ahead keys when they start (sudo, ssh) drop keys sent before they read. What you type after a password prompt appears is never shown or saved.
- The agent's own terminal is the first one listed in its Computer tab, already open, and it is where the agent's commands show. Each terminal row shows its folder and the last command, or Idle.
- One door: the chat pane has no separate Terminal tab beside Computer, and no Attach anywhere; terminals are attached by themselves. A person's terminals read "Your terminals" or "Sara, terminals" instead of "own tab".
- After a terminal ends, its last screen stays as it was; a failed or empty read never replaces it and the screen is not polled again.
- A terminal row from the list carries the chat it was opened from (threadId).
- A terminal that ended (you typed exit) is learned by the open sheet at once: the bar says "This terminal ended" with New tab, the keyboard is released, and the last screen stays. A terminal row shows the host's own state (Running while a command runs).
