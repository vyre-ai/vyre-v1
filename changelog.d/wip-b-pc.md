- An agent's Computer tab is a list of its terminals and one picture of its browser. There is no Attach button: the agent's terminal is already open when you look, and the terminals of its helpers and the people working in it are rows too, each with what it last printed. Press a row to open that terminal full screen.
- Press the browser picture in the Computer tab to open the browser full screen, live. Take over and Give back for the browser are there, not in the tab. A sleeping browser wakes when you press its picture. Closing the browser while you hold it hands it back.
- In the full-screen terminal you watch what an agent's terminal does: its screen, who it belongs to and its ended and reconnecting states. Typing into an agent's terminal is not available yet in this release (taking over is switched off); a person cannot take the keyboard, so there is no Take over and no Give back.
- The Computer tab lost its "Asleep" chip, its explanation lines, its Steps list and the paragraph about the older desktop. Each state is a dot and one word. Signed-in sites show only when there are some.
- A person's own terminal tab is named for them: "You" and "Your tab" for yourself, their name for everyone else. The full-screen terminal has one bar.
- While a person has taken over the browser, only they see its picture; everyone else sees who is using it. The browser picture in the Computer tab no longer keeps a sleeping browser awake.
- The full-screen terminal and the Computer tab say what is wrong in each fault, with its one action: a terminal that ended (New tab), a connection that dropped (Reconnecting, the last lines stay), a computer starting, a browser that stopped (Restart).
- Text typed into a terminal before a program has turned its echo off is echoed, and programs that clear typed-ahead keys when they start (sudo, ssh) drop keys sent before they read. What you type after a password prompt appears is never shown or saved.
- The agent's own terminal is the first one listed in its Computer tab, already open, and it is where the agent's commands show. Each terminal row shows its folder and the last command, or Idle.
- One door: the chat pane has no separate Terminal tab beside Computer, and no Attach anywhere; terminals are attached by themselves. A person's terminals read "Your terminals" or "Sara, terminals" instead of "own tab".
- After a terminal ends, its last screen stays as it was; a failed or empty read never replaces it and the screen is not polled again.
- A terminal row from the list carries the chat it was opened from (threadId).
- A terminal that ended (you typed exit) is learned by the open sheet at once: the bar says "This terminal ended" with New tab, and the last screen stays. A terminal row shows the host's own state (Running while a command runs).
- A person watching a terminal where the agent ran a command sees the command and its output, never the line that types it (markers, session id): it reads as the bare command on every screen, read and replay.
- Typing into an agent's terminal is off in this release (re-sent keys are not safe to repeat yet): the full-screen terminal is a watching view with no Take over; the ended and reconnecting states stay. The browser picture is withheld when the check of who holds the browser fails.
- With typing off, every kind of caller is refused (not only people), a hold stored before it went off blocks nobody, and the terminal sheet only watches until the workspace answer says typing is on.
- Where no computer can run a terminal (a Personal Space with no Docker, no image and no other computer of yours), the Computer tab says "No computer to open a terminal on. Open Vyre on a computer, or add a server." in place of the Terminals list, and draws no terminal row and no sleeping browser picture. A terminal row that does open nothing for another reason says why in one line under Terminals.
