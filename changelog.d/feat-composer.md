One new composer in every chat, Ask pane and Inbox reply. The bar shows who will answer only when an AI will: the agent's face and name, its provider's logo, the model, the account's name when you have two of one provider, and effort bars. People-only chats show nothing until you @mention an agent. Click the strip for the model panel: pick the account (only the ones you connected, by the names you gave them, with Add another AI provider), the model, effort (keys 1 to 4), see context used, session and week limits for that account with their reset times (a number the provider does not report is left out), switch to your least used account in one tap when one runs low, set the chat's mode, and turn Switch when limits run out on or off. It stays open while you pick. The mode chip (Ask first, Auto, Plan) cycles with Shift Tab. A ring by Send shows how full the context is once it passes half. While an agent works the top edge glows, Send turns into Stop, and typing splits it into Queue and Now (Option Enter). # searches records, files, folders, chats, automations, projects and channels at once, grouped, with a preview of the one you are on, and writes an inline token; a folder files the chat in it. @ and / menus show faces, roles and commands with skills. Attachments sit in a tray with their sizes; From Files attaches a file you already keep. Inbox replies have To with an Email and Text switch, a From list of every mailbox, sending domain and number grouped by service with its logo (and a way to connect another), Subject for email only, and Command Enter to send email.
