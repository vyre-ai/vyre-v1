- Meetings now fill from Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. After each member's mailbox pass, an ended conference or Teams meeting with a transcript lands as a Meeting (merged with its calendar event on the join link) and its transcript, with speakers and times, is attached. Meet is read as the member; Teams is read as the meeting's organizer, only when the organizer is a mapped and included member, so an excluded member's meetings are never read. The Microsoft access-policy script now includes the Teams transcript policy for each included member.
