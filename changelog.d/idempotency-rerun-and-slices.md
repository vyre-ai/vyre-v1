A page read from an outside app (connectors.read) is no longer kept for a repeat of its key: the repeat asks again. Other answers over 64 KB are kept as a compact record of the ids and fields they named. The daily purge of expired answers runs in slices of a few milliseconds, a new events.db hands freed pages back in steps, and erasing an agent's answers or a person's words no longer vacuums a big live log. On a firm's server the answers kept for sync pages were 33 GB of a 34 GB log.
