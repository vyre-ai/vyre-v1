A chat that needs your Vault key now waits for it instead of failing at once. Right after the app starts the key arrives a moment later, and if it is still locked after 45 seconds the chat shows Unlock, which opens in place. Signing in to Claude or Codex again now refreshes the account you already have instead of adding a second "Claude Code 2" beside it. Extra copies from before stay where they are; to tidy them, delete the extra items in the Vault and keep the one your chats use.

On the Mac app, opening Vyre shows a lock screen first when your Vault key is not held: your name, your password, Unlock. After that one entry there are no more locks or password prompts in that session. If the background service restarts while the app is open, the app hands the key back by itself, before the next chat turn needs it. The key is kept in the app's memory only and never written to disk; sign-out and quitting drop it. After an update you see the lock screen once. Phone and web are unchanged.
