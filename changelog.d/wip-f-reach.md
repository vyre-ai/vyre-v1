- What your agent makes for you is yours, and the agent that made it can keep using it. Before, an agent could make a site, an automation, a form, a channel, a project or a page for you but not change or remove it afterwards, could not put a folder in your Personal files, and could not add an event to your Personal calendar. Now the same agent, acting for you, can read, change and remove what it made for you, and can work in your Personal files and calendar. It stays private to you unless you share it, and nobody else gains anything. Handing out access, and anything in your Vault, stay with you.
- An agent's calendar event is no longer held for your OK unless it invites someone; before, every event an agent added waited. You could not open an event you had made through the API at all; now you can. An agent's archive of a channel or a project waits for your OK like its other deletes.
- An agent's change to an event on a Google or Outlook calendar now waits for your OK, whether or not it invites anyone, because it goes out in your name. Events on your own Vyre calendar do not wait.
