- Memory learns on its own by default: a new agent's capture mode is auto (everything it learns is saved), and every learned fact shows in the Learned feed (memory.learned) with its source and who learned it, with one-call Undo (memory.undo). A Space can require review for shared memory, for the whole Space or for named projects, and then Space and project facts wait in Proposed.
- Deleting or merging away a fact moves it to Trash instead of removing it: not recalled and not in normal search, found with Trash search, and restored in one call by any person or agent who can see it (memory.restore, also an agent tool). Facts untouched in Trash for the Space period (30 to 90 days, default 60) move to Archive each night, out of all search and restorable by an admin. Erase still removes content and leaves a contentless line.
- Every change to a fact (create, edit, merge, delete, restore, archive, erase) is recorded on a trail that is never deleted (memory.trail): who, what, when, why and source.
- Agents and other non-person callers can now read Space-wide settings.
