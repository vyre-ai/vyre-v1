- Memory learns on its own by default: a new agent's capture mode is auto (everything it learns is saved), and every learned fact shows in the Learned feed (memory.learned) with its source and who learned it, with one-call Undo (memory.undo). A Space can require review for shared memory, for the whole Space or for named projects, and then Space and project facts wait in Proposed.
- Deleting or merging away a fact moves it to Trash instead of removing it: not recalled and not in normal search, found with Trash search, and restored in one call by any person or agent who can see it (memory.restore, also an agent tool). Facts untouched in Trash for the Space period (30 to 90 days, default 60) move to Archive each night, out of all search and restorable by an admin. Erase still removes content and leaves a contentless line.
- Every change to a fact (create, edit, merge, delete, restore, archive, erase) is recorded on a trail that is never deleted (memory.trail): who, what, when, why and source.
- Agents and other non-person callers can now read Space-wide settings.
- Upkeep does real work: exact and near-duplicate facts are merged (the old rows go to Trash), two values for one subject and field and facts that are old for their kind become questions with one-click answers, and when a memory key is available a model merges by meaning, writes pattern facts, learns from corrections and tidies pages. Every action is on the trail; each run records its tokens and cost, and the monthly cap stops model work.
- Meaning search: a small local model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, fetched on first use into the Space data folder and checked against pinned hashes) finds facts, notes and pages by meaning and is blended with keyword and trigram search. Search says in plain words when it is keyword-only; a Space setting switches it to the memory key's provider or off.
- Living pages: a page per subject with versioned Notes, [[links]] that become labelled edges with the line they were written in, backlinks from chats, runs, records and other pages, graph reads with typed nodes (chats included), edge evidence, an as-of date, layer and type filters and a local view, and a Markdown export of everything the reader can see. Personal pages stay sealed to their owner.
