- Every trigger and step in the Automations builder can be set up. A trigger's Configure tab now has a form (name, record type, form, spreadsheet, repository and so on) where it used to say only "This is trigger 3 of 3". Seven steps whose input arrived as a bare reference (Copy, Move and Share a file, Get a pull request, List Gmail messages, Get sheet values, Relate records) now list their fields, and every other step gets a text box, number, list, choice, yes or no, person picker or JSON box for each property, with the required ones first and the rest under More options.
- A schedule has a real picker: every N minutes or hours, day, weekday, week (day chips) or month, at a time, in a time zone that starts as the one in Settings with a Change link. It shows the schedule in words ("Every Monday at 09:00, Asia/Karachi") and the next 3 runs. A cron line and the missed-run rule sit under Advanced. A new schedule starts as every day at 09:00; a schedule with no time is an error on the card.
- A card says what it does: a trigger names its record type and event, form, repository or schedule; a step shows its recipient or the first words of its prompt, and "Not set up" in amber when it lacks something it needs. "Every time" is gone.
- The step panel's tabs carry the name and icon of their card ("On a schedule", "Form submitted", "Send the digest") instead of three tabs called Trigger. At most four stay open and a deleted step closes its tab. Picking another card while the panel is open shows it in the panel.
- Dead controls fixed: the trigger panel can remove its trigger; the Test and Data tabs of a trigger explain themselves; Advanced shows a trigger as JSON; the empty bar under the tabs has what to do next and an Undo button; Events moved under More as Live events; the add-trigger menu no longer shows ticks.
- The step catalog now returns each input schema with its references in place (automations.steps.list).
