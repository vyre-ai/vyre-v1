- In a chat with an agent your own message has a "Rewind to here" icon on its hover bar and in its menu, and "/rewind" does the same. A card "Go back to" lists the points; Go back puts the agent's files back, folds the later messages into one line you can open, and Undo brings it all back. In a chat with other people you go back only to your own message, and everyone sees one line that says who did it.
