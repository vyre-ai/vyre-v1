# Home (home)

Lane L6. Kind module. Version 0.1.0.

Requires: notifications, runs, tasks.

## Capabilities (8)

- [home.activity](../capabilities/home.activity.md): See all: the full activity for the digest period on records I follow, my agents, and my routines
- [home.at_risk](../capabilities/home.at_risk.md): Projects at risk: overdue, blocked or slipped, with the counting rule
- [home.checklist](../capabilities/home.checklist.md): The first-run checklist: five items, hidden once all are done or dismissed
- [home.digest](../capabilities/home.digest.md): My While you were away digest: three lines, then at most seven ranked items, each decision with Undo
- [home.dismiss_checklist](../capabilities/home.dismiss_checklist.md): Dismiss the first-run checklist
- [home.mark_digest_seen](../capabilities/home.mark_digest_seen.md): Mark the digest seen so it resets
- [home.routines_today](../capabilities/home.routines_today.md): Today's routines of my agents: scheduled, running, done or failed, one line each
- [home.write_digest](../capabilities/home.write_digest.md): Store a digest the assistant wrote for a person (scheduled)

## Events (3)

- [checklist.dismissed](../events/checklist.dismissed.md): The first-run checklist was dismissed or finished
- [digest.seen](../events/digest.seen.md): A person saw their digest; it resets
- [digest.written](../events/digest.written.md): The While you were away digest was written for a person
